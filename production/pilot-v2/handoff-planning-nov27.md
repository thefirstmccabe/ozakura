# Handoff to Planning: consider moving the inbox and the sweep up (Nov 27)

**From:** the scene-list thread (Story role). **Needs the author:** no new decision; he raised it himself (below).

## What happened
On Nov 27 a small decision was settled in one live Story thread after a second Story thread had already applied that day's round. The author carried it across by pasting a message, and asked:

> "I thought in the earlier planning thread we found a way for me to stop having to be the messenger between threads. Is that not implemented, or is this an exception?"

He was told it isn't implemented: the inbox and the scheduled sweep are §6 of `handoff-planning-nov22.md` and step 6 of its §9, Planning is on step 3, and there is no `production/handoffs/` folder.

## The ask
Consider building the inbox and the sweep before step 3, or alongside it. The order of work is yours and his to set.

Story offered him this note; his reply was "Leave it," which Story read as "leave the note." If that reading was wrong, delete this file and the pointer in the Nov 22 handoff's §9.

## What the case shows, for the design
1. **It was the sweep's exact case:** one line for canon, no author decision needed.
2. **A sweep doesn't reach a thread that is live.** The stopgap used here was a "Pending for the next Story session" line in the deliverable's brief (`production/scene-list/README.md`, Status). Worth a rule that a live thread pulls and checks the inbox before it tells the author it is done.
3. **Why the first thread didn't just make the edit:** uploading a living doc to the Project means reading the whole doc into the thread first, and canon is large. A sweep session pays that once for a batch.
4. **Two Story-role threads were live at once,** one finishing a discussion and one applying it. The ownership table doesn't say which holds canon in that overlap. One option: once a handover is consumed, the older thread stops editing `story/` and writes only to the deliverable's notes or the inbox.
