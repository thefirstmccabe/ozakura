## Context
- Day one of second year (Monday, April 7), first person: Kōhei Fujisawa, 16, who carries his house. Dad died last spring, Mom stays behind the closed fusuma, and his sister Akane (14, middle school) is often unwell. Next door, three feet away, is Nanoha Tachibana, his childhood friend, who loves him and hides it. Mizuno Natsuki transfers in that day.
- The chapter opens with a wordless cold open (P.0: a misty flash-forward with no voices) and ends with his recurring dream (1.8): a woman in another life binding his cut wrist, "You're late." Her face is never shown.
- **The reader-knowledge target pulls two ways.** Both girls must read as real love interests, and Kōhei is visibly drawn to both even where he doesn't name it. Who the dream woman is must stay divided (no line may point at either girl). **Natsuki has to be as likable as Nanoha**: the Oct 17 readers loved Nanoha's lines and found Natsuki's padded, so spend the effort on her charm (§3b: she asks, pokes, notices physical details; no verdicts or self-labels). Akane must sound like herself, not a younger Nanoha.
