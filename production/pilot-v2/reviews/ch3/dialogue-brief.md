**Context.** Translated-manga VN, Kōhei's first-person present-tense narration (Nanoha's and Natsuki's interludes are third person; Ch3 has none). Spring of second year, week one: Monday Ch1 (Natsuki transfers in), Tuesday–Wednesday Ch2 (his shift; kendo; the catch; the bridge invitation "That's my project, then"). Ch3 is Thursday night and Saturday. Kōhei carries the house (Dad died last spring, Mom stays behind the fusuma, Akane is frail). Nanoha is the neighbor since birth, privately deadpan, publicly gentle; she loves him and hides it. Natsuki is warm, quick, competitive, a transfer with six schools behind her.
