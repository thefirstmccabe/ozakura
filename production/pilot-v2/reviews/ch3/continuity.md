# Ch3 "Windows": continuity review

Independent continuity editor, Nov 1. Line numbers refer to `production/pilot-v2/script/ch03.ozs` unless another file is named.

## Must-fix

1. **Takatsuki explains away Natsuki's reading.** Line 136: "Your left hand drops before you step in. Mizuno has noticed." This gives her a conscious cue that can be named. It contradicts Ch2's interlude (`ch02.ozs:409`, "She didn't know how she'd known where he would move") and Takatsuki's own line (`ch02.ozs:267`): after a year facing Kōhei he has never been there first, so a visible tell isn't something he'd miss. It also blunts fix 6. **Fix:** point it at her established fault (`ch02.ozs:251`). The joke stays and the one-sidedness gets sharper: "Fujisawa. Mizuno still drops her left shoulder before {men|head strikes}. You did not see it once. I am telling you so that it is fair." / ふじさわ。みずのは、めんを打つ前に、まだ左肩が下がる。一度も気づいていなかった。公平のために言っておく。 Lines 137–138 stand as written.

2. **The outline's "Neither mentions Saturday" (3.2) is broken.** In lines 105–107 Nanoha asks "Practice Saturday?" and gets "Then the shrine." She already knows the plan (`ch01.ozs:148–151`), so the question reads as fishing. It also gives Kōhei an opening, which makes the secret louder than the outline wants. **Fix:** cut the three lines. Line 108 works directly after "We don't discuss it."

3. **Akane's address breaks §3c in both columns.** Line 310: "So you're Mizuno-san." / あなたが、みずのさん？ The sheet has Akane say "Natsuki-san" (changing to "-chan" in Ch15), and Akane never uses *anata*. **Fix:** "So you're Natsuki-san." / なつきさん、だよね？

4. **Practice length contradicts Ch2.** Line 121: "Saturday practice is three hours." Ch2 has "Four days a week, for two hours" (`ch02.ozs:122`), and pilot-notes §3 has "practices run two hours." **Fix:** "Saturday practice is like any Saturday: warm-ups, practice swings, drills until my forearms burn."

5. **"See you Monday" collides with Ch4.** Line 328. In the outline Natsuki studies at the Fujisawa house on Sunday (4.1). **Fix:** "See you, Fujisawa! Progress reports!" / またね、ふじさわ！ 進捗報告、忘れないでよ！ Alternatively, seed the study date here.
