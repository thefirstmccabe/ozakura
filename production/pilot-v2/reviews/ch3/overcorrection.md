Independent reviewer, Nov 1 (§9 step 3c, part 3). Read: style guide §3b–§3c, the dialogue brief and the outline's Ch3 section, the diagnosis, draft 1 (before) and `script/ch03.ozs` (after). The script is unedited.

## 1. Livelier, or just shorter?

Mostly livelier. The revision is ten lines shorter, and most of the cut lines are narration that explained or vouched for the scene (0121, 0132, 0338, 0427, 0307). Natsuki gains the most:
- "You lift your right heel before you feint. Every time." (0331) uses her own mechanism, and it makes Takatsuki's "I am telling you so that it is fair." (0332) land literally.
- "But I smell like a kendo glove." (0279) and "Who's Mio? Is she good?" (0281) are hers.
- The Niigata socks story (0350) is a real laugh that comes out of her history.

Akane's "Then kneel somewhere else. I'm asleep." (0073) is cheerful defiance, not a younger Nanoha. 0383 and 0423 now show instead of tell.

Most losses are small and worth it: "His behavior or mine?" / "We don't discuss it." (0316–0317), "a broom and an opinion" (0129), "The system has failed him" (0112), "Kendo's." (0334), "though I'm not sure whose" (0309) and Mio's "decided to keep" (0427). The window keeps its whole bit and ends more cleanly on 0315. Two losses matter:
- Natsuki's "Whose side are you on?" was cut along with Takatsuki's quip, so all she has left is "Takatsuki-kun!" (0333).
- The tea scene lost Kōhei's only question to her about the branch (0392–0394).

## 2. Overcorrection

- **Natsuki swung from Nanoha's memo jokes to stock dares.** "With progress reports. I bet you can't keep it up for a month." (0348) and "Make me." (0364) are two taunts in 17 lines. "Make me." is the worst line in the revision. It's a generic English comeback. With 0365 now ending "waiting to see what I'll do," it reads as practiced flirting (her Never column) and tips the wanting toward her, which the outline says Ch3 must not do.
- **Her sincere line still has two closers.** The diagnosis asked for the history laugh to be the only one after 0346. The revision kept a joke at 0348 (now a dare) and added a one-month bet that isn't in the outline.
- **Kōhei isn't blank.** Every straight-man line survives ("I'm kneeling.", "Your authority is a plushie.", "The station's the other way.", "…Four and a half.", "Traitors. Both of you."). Cutting 0316 was right, since it said the subtext out loud. **Nanoha isn't timid.** Her bit is intact, and the window ends on her non-answer, as the brief asks. Apart from "Make me.", nothing reads as sitcom.
- Natsuki now reads three of his tells (heel, sleep, left hand). That fits "Ch3's face-reader is Natsuki," but three is the limit.

## 5. Read-aloud

- "and she catches me catching myself, and that's it. I'm gone." (0358) sounds written. "I'm gone" can mean "I've fallen for her" as easily as "I cracked up," and Ch3 can't carry that.
- "So does Mizuno, for about two seconds." (0368) has nothing to point back to. The "stop walking" it answers is four lines and a CG earlier (0181).
- Minor, leave: "she gives me the same look she gave me" (0352); "in both hands, solemnly, at arm's length" (0101).
