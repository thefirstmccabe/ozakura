**Strange / unexplained:**

**What kind of story:** a slice-of-life school romance with a grief undercurrent (dead or missing dad, withdrawn mom, sick sister, a boy who over-functions). The prologue promises a supernatural or fog-mystery turn. Title-wise, the tree will matter.

The lesson is a cute idea, but the triple-explaining flattens it into a glossary.
4. **Auntie Emi (part 12):** "Barging? Nonsense! Come in, come in, before everything gets cold. Akane-chan's already here." Stock anime mom. The running gag about her ("She can't measure burdock. It's a sickness.") is funnier than she is in person. Give her one specific line.
5. **Natsuki / Kōhei (part 5):** "Is the mountain always that close? From the station it looks like you could walk there." / "It's farther than it looks." Generic small talk with no fingerprint. Everything else Natsuki says in that scene is specific. The reply also reads as a heavy thematic hint if that's what it's meant to be.
6. **Classmate (part 5):** "Nacchan, you have to get the strawberry parfait. Everybody gets the parfait." Filler. The nameless classmates all speak with one voice.
7. **Natsuki / Kōhei (part 7):** "Fujisawa! Race you!" / "It's not a race." The most stock exchange in the read. The scene recovers immediately ("I go after her. Of course I go after her.").
8. **Nanoha (part 9):** "You're not the only person who loves her, Kōhei." Not flat exactly, since I liked it, but it states the theme outright. The scene had already shown it with the sleeve in the photo and "I know. That's why I didn't call." This could be one notch quieter.

### With names covered: could I tell them apart?

Mostly yes. The strongest voices:
- **Takatsuki:** no contractions, formal and precise ("It is the hardest thing in kendo to do on purpose"). Impossible to miss.
- **Rika:** exclamations, repetition, "Oh! Oh oh oh."
- **Mio:** third-person "Mio is the best in this house."
- **Grandpa:** "Good, good." / "Ha! This one'll do."
- **The manager:** one line, used nine-plus times, and it works.
- **Nanoha:** dry and mock-bureaucratic ("paperwork," "a folder in the staff room," "customarily," "his sentence has been reduced"). She's stiff and stammery with strangers ("Um—", "I'm working up to it"), which is a nice split.
- **Natsuki:** quick and breezy, with imperatives and exclamations ("Hand me a rag," "Race you!", "Eh, 'san'? We're the same age!"). She observes bodies ("Left palm," "You lift your right heel").

Where they blur:
- **The "Then [reframe]" comeback is a house tic shared by nearly everyone.** Nanoha: "Then I've arrived." Akane: "Then go hover over the eggs!" and "Then kneel somewhere else." Natsuki: "Then one kids' plate, please," "Then I'm a spiritual first-year," "Then I'm taking the long way." Rika: "Then we've already narrowed it down!" Ishida: "Then by the time you get there, it's cold." With names covered, any of those could belong to any of them.
- **Nanoha and Natsuki in "observing Kōhei" mode** are interchangeable. "You smiled." / "That one's new." (Nanoha) vs. "Four hours." / "And you keep shaking out your left hand." (Natsuki). Both read his body and tell him about it. That's thematically intentional, but it erases the difference between the voices in those lines.
- **Akane sounds like a younger Nanoha.** Same deadpan, same two-against-one teasing. That's plausible, since Nanoha basically half-raised her, but it's still a blur.
- **Kōhei and Nanoha share a register** (dry, short, deadpan). That fits people who grew up together, but in quick back-and-forths I sometimes had to check the speaker tag.
- **Kirishima and Takatsuki** are both laconic. Takatsuki's lack of contractions keeps them apart, but Kirishima has less of his own.

### Mismatches between text and illustrations

1. **image_12 (significant):** the text says the low branch is "at shoulder height... low enough that I almost duck." The art puts the long branch well above head height, far over Natsuki. The supernatural "the branch is lower than it should be" beat depends on that normal height being low.
2. **image_13 (significant):** the "small point of light under the low branch" sits on the horizon over the mountains and reads as the sun peeking over a ridge, in what is a midday scene. It isn't clearly under the branch or clearly unnatural. It undercuts "…You came back."
3. **image_14 (significant):** a silver-lavender-haired girl at the table is, by elimination, Akane. Her appearance is never described in the text, and nobody comments on it. I had to stop and work out who she was. Nanoha, with her hair down and no braid or clip, is hard to recognize compared with image_04 and image_10/11.
4. **image_11 (minor):** after she "turns Usagi-san a quarter turn, so he faces the corner of the window frame," the art still shows the rabbit with its back squarely to us. The last beat ("the rabbit is still facing the corner") isn't what's drawn.
5. **image_04 (minor):** "Nanoha walks on the river side," but in the art the river is on Kōhei's side. They've stopped and are facing each other, so this is forgivable.
6. **image_07 (minor/ambiguous):** the cut is described as "on the inside of" the wrist, but the blood spot reads as being on the back or outer side of the wrist.

### Would I keep reading?

**Yes.** Honestly, more than I expected after part 1.

**What hooked me:**
- "…You came back," and the snow smell under the branch. That's the moment it stopped being only cozy.
- Both heroines are decent to each other. Neither is a villain or an obstacle, so whichever way it goes, it'll hurt. The rabbit-in-time-out scene, re-read through "Thursday. After school. Before the window.", is the best piece of writing in the read.
- The restraint about grief: the whiteboard, the trash on the wrong day, "It has been off center for a year."
- The side cast earns its space. Takatsuki, Rika, the manager and Mio all get laughs in a line or two.

**What would make me stop:**
- If the supernatural stays in "almost" mode much longer. Three chapters gave me one on-screen event. Chapter 4 needs to move it.
- If Akane's condition turns into a terminal-illness tearjerker. The withholding is building toward something, and I'm wary of what.
- If the symmetry stays perfect. Two of everything, both girls endlessly gracious, nobody ever wanting anything badly enough to be a little selfish. At some point one of them needs to want something out loud, at the other's expense.
- If the prologue (fog and katana) doesn't come back soon. Right now it's a promise the story hasn't touched in three chapters.

**Small craft notes:** the translated-manga localization uses feet and miles ("ten feet," "three feet of night," "two hundred miles"). That's standard for localized manga, but slightly at odds with the dense translator's notes, which signal a more foreignizing translation. The translator's notes are well written, but in the dojo scene they double up on in-dialogue glosses.
