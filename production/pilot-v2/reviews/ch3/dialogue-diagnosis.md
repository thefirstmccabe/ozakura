## 3. Scene-level problems

**Buttons.** Yes, too many: 14 exchanges end on a punchline or quotable tag (0088, 0118, 0126, 0317, 0323, 0334, 0340, 0364, 0391, 0274, 0281, 0292, 0421, 0308). Some are required or good (0118, 0274, 0340); the problem is the density. The best moments are the unbuttoned ones: "…Window." (0094), the silence after 0130, "…Four and a half." (0356), the rain dodge (0401–0404), "A petal." (0266), 0275.

**Feelings stated or shown.** The dialogue keeps feelings off the surface well. The stating happens in the narration (0121, 0132, 0323, 0375, 0427, 0307–0308), so fix the narration, not the dialogue.

**Humor mechanisms (§3b).** Kōhei is right except 0316; Nanoha is right (a committed bit) but stretched (0101, 0112, 0317); Genji, Mio and Kirishima are right; Emi is right except 0285; Takatsuki's 0332 is perfect and 0334 is a quip; Akane drifts toward Nanoha at 0073. Natsuki uses her own mechanism only at 0355–0357, 0390, 0399 and 0425; elsewhere her jokes are Nanoha's paperwork deadpan (0348, 0364) or generic (0331, 0281, 0291).

**Narration** repeats or vouches for the dialogue at 0085, 0101, 0121, 0132, 0181, 0268, 0338, 0375, 0423, 0427 and 0307. Staging note for Production: 0398 says "in the same breath," but 0396 and 0397 put Nanoha's line after his.

**Tics.** "very" is clean (only the required 0379); "She says it…" appears once (0347). "the way she…" appears four times (0352, 0383, 0423, 0430), over the limit: keep 0430's arm-wave callback and vary the rest. Akane's "don't make that face" (0087) is a light second face-read, tolerable. The translated feel breaks at 0112, 0129 and 0291. 0338, 0350 and 0383 reuse Ch2 material.

**Natsuki vs. Nanoha: not even; Nanoha is ahead.** Nanoha gets the best-built scene, the most gracious acts (the broom, the invitation) and the mystery, and her sincere line gets a silence and narration weight (0131). Natsuki's sincere line (0346) has two jokes stacked on it within four lines, and six of her lines fail the blind test. Her best material (0355–0357, 0276–0277, 0390, 0428) shows she can carry the chapter. The gap is line-level, not structural: fix 0348, 0350, 0364, 0393, 0281 and 0291 and she's level.

**Brief compliance.** Every required beat is present. Two checks: 0314 and 0164 leave Usagi-san "facing the corner," close to Ch4's facing-the-wall payoff (Story should confirm it doesn't take that beat early); and kohei(deadpan) sits on two set-up lines, 0102 and 0339, against the punchline-only rule (Production).

## 4. Five highest-value fixes

1. Put Natsuki's jokes back in her own voice: keep "progress reports" but deliver it as a dare, not a memo (0348, 0364), and make the history laugh the only closer after 0346, from a town other than Sapporo (0350).
2. Cut the narration that explains or vouches: 0121, 0132, 0338, 0375, 0427, 0307, and the ends of 0268 and 0181.
3. Let at least four exchanges end without a button: stop the window scene at 0315, drop "Kendo's" or make it a full literal sentence, end the invitation on Natsuki asking about Mio, and end the chapter on 0306.
4. Give Natsuki her own way of dodging her unease at the branch: cut 0392–0394, or have her get brighter and busier when he asks, never flat.
5. Fix the photo continuity at 0362: take the photo at the laugh (0351), or change what it catches.
