# Ch2 continuity report — `production/pilot-v2/script/ch02.ozs`

## Problems, most important first

**1. Too many pointers to Natsuki as the dream woman (target: 60% at most).** The ledger gives Ch2 two (the match read; the catch and tear). With the memory cue at the catch on top, the draft adds three more:
- **c2_0278–0279** "like the next step in the dark in a house she'd lived in all her life. / She had never lived anywhere all her life." The chapter's loudest "she's the wife" line; it brushes the outline's "not yet: fragments of her own." Fix: end at "She hadn't read him. She'd just known." Cut c2_0279, or replace it with "She didn't like not knowing how."
- **c2_0115** Takatsuki: "Most people can't do it reliably until fourth dan." This makes her kote read as uncanny, and it isn't plausible: debana-kote is a staple of high-school kendo, and high-schoolers top out at third dan. Cut it, or use "It is the hardest thing to do on purpose."
- **c2_0168** "Luck doesn't explain it." It pushes the outline's beat (c2_0166, "never once been there first") on to "inexplicable." Fix, in his voice: "Luck is not a technique."

**2. The insert makes the recognition mutual (fix 6; canon §5).** The panel before c2_0148 reads "Kōhei is already under it." Cold readers get the panel text, and "already" says he anticipated her. Fix: "Kōhei drops under it as it comes." "This time I don't think" is fine (instinct).

**3. The first-years contradict "one new member."** c2_0070 "One new member, a second-year transfer" (新入部員が一人いる) can't stand next to first-years in the room on April 9 (c2_0072, c2_0079, c2_0174). Fix, keeping the jokes: "Welcome back. First-years start trials next week. One new member already: a second-year transfer. Mizuno." Then:
- c2_0072 → "a couple of the third-years laugh"
- c2_0079 → "You'll take the first-years when they come, Takatsuki…"
- c2_0174 → "Until the first-years join, whoever stays does the floor. I usually stay anyway."

The new c2_0174 also explains why only two of them mop.

**4. His pull toward her is named instead of shown** (chapter map: "plainly drawn… unnamed").
- **c2_0075** "I like her more for that than I want to." Cut it; "Old equipment. Well loved." already does the work.
- **c2_0251** "I find I don't mind." That's acceptance, and canon has him avoid the pull. Dodge it practically (§3b): "I find I don't mind. It's only trash bags."
- Optional: **c2_0046** "Then they get careful with me" states what the scene already shows.

With Nanoha absent from his side of Ch2, these are the lines that tip readers to "Natsuki wins."

**5. Kendo.**
- **c2_0166** "on his wrist before he'd lifted" contradicts the panel ("as his arms come up") and misdefines debana, which happens *as* he lifts. Fix: "the moment he lifted" (振りかぶった瞬間に).
- **The panel before c2_0145**, "laughing, out of breath" / "She's laughing": she has just taught "No celebrating" (c2_0106). Make it eyes smiling behind the grille, still in zanshin ("She's smiling. Of course she is."), or give Kirishima the note in the debrief.
- **c2_0136** "Then bogu. The men goes on last." Kirikaeshi and waza came before the break, so dō and tare are already on, and the kote go on after the men. Fix: "After the break, the men and kote go back on…"
- Nits: unwaxed floors (c2_0059); sleeves not pushed up (c2_0074); panel-1 referee to the side, no arm at hajime.

**6. Kirishima's sprite.** **c2_0081** `kirishima<laugh>: "That was a joke."` His sheet is deadpan ("nobody laughs, and he doesn't wait for it"). Keep him on `calm`.

**7. Address and speech.**
- **c2_0156** "Captain." || はい。 and **c2_0158** "…Yes, Captain." || ……はい。: the voiced lines drop 主将. Use はい、主将。, as Natsuki's c2_0154 does.
- **c2_0128** 教材くん is the only -kun she puts on him. Her bare surname for him, against "-kun" for the other boys, is a planted signal, so consider いい感じだよ、教材。
- Takatsuki should use few contractions. Change c2_0166 "didn't… he'd" and c2_0168 "doesn't" to the full forms.
- **c2_0245** 大桜: the header keeps names in kana until their kanji are chosen (fix 14). Use おおざくら.

**8. Staging.**

- The insert runs 3 pages and 10 panels; the outline budgets 2 pages and about 9. The dō on a new page is right (§4a); update Needs and the cost.

**9. Akane's routine against Ch3.** c2_0009 sets up a photo of the empty container at seven on shift nights, but Ch3 3.1 has him take "nothing from Akane" as good news. Hand this to Ch3: either play the missing photo as the wrong note, or make c2_0009 a one-off.
