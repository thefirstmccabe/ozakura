# Ch2 dialogue rewrite: overcorrection check

**Verdict: ship the revision with the fixes in §6.** The dialogue is better: it's more distinct, Natsuki asks more often, the Nanoha deadpan is gone from her lines, and the lesson plays as a game instead of a glossary. But the cuts to narration overshot on Kōhei. Twice she asks him something directly and he says nothing, and most of his interior responses to her between the dojo and the bridge are gone. That's the "Kōhei goes blank" failure again, spread thinly across the chapter instead of sitting in one line. A few of her warm or charming lines went with them.

## 1. Livelier, or just shorter and drier?

**Dialogue: livelier.** These lines are real gains:
- "Who do I get to fight first?" (0071), which Kirishima's 0077 now answers.
- The "Me-e-en!" demonstration, and Rika's "So the yelling is you telling them where." becoming her own inference.
- The pop quiz (0108).
- "Hoshikawa. Don't draw that." (0127), which pays off the sketchbook.
- "It's not a race." (0181), which makes "I go after her" funnier.
- "Are your ears always that red?" (0207) in place of "Smooth."
- "It's mostly vending machines." (0218) on its own, the Ch1 sincere-then-laugh model done exactly.
- "That's nine." moved ahead of the double take.

**Narration: drier, and the losses all fall on Kōhei's response to Natsuki:**
- 0019 "I've never once thought about it." (the effect her noticing has on him)
- 0163 "I understand it completely." (kinship, which is what she offers him)
- 0228 "So I say the only thing I've got." (he's trying to meet her bid)
- 0234 "…longer than I'd like her to see." (he's self-conscious under her eyes)
- 0251 "I find I don't mind."

Each cut is defensible alone. Together, from the dojo to the bridge, Kōhei registers her only in the race and the catch.

The cuts I agree with: 0045 (it broke the brief), 0058 (0057's rough throat lands it), 0075 (it named the pull, and "Old equipment. Well loved." is stronger alone), 0251 ("It's only trash bags." alone is better, and ominous), 0067, 0165 and 0173.

**Lost good lines:**
- 0163, her joy and his kinship with it.
- 0201's doubled-down bluff, "It's a thing. Look it up." It was her getting *more* entertaining when hurt; "Late bloomers." alone is a limp tag.
- 0284's coin-eating vending machine. It's the one place Nanoha's Ch1 bit lives on in Natsuki's own voice, and it makes the cheerful text specific. This is the closest thing here to the "cut Nanoha's one joke" failure.
- 0279 "She didn't like not knowing how." This is the brief's "unsettled by how well she read him." Without it, the interlude states the uncanny fact and moves straight to Chie with no reaction from her.
- Minor: 0104 "Exactly." (her mischief in egging Rika on), 0129 "like the answer printed at the back of the book", and 0145's zanshin callback. I'd let these go.

## 2. Overcorrection

- **Kōhei blank:** yes, at 0092 and 0207. "How do I look?" and "Are your ears always that red?" are direct questions to him, and neither gets a reply, a face window or a narrated reaction. On the page they read as dropped lines. These are also exactly the moments where his pull should show and stay unnamed. Cutting "Shut up." (0208) was right, but the slot needed his literal answer, not silence.
- **Natsuki pushy?** "Who do I get to fight first?" sits near the edge for a formal lineup. It fits her Ch1 "please don't go easy on me," and the third-years' laugh covers it. Keep it.
- **Natsuki timid?** No. "Incomplete how?" (0131) is plain but generous to Takatsuki, and Kirishima's cut-in keeps it moving.
- **Natsuki generic:** "How do I look?" is a line anyone could say. It works once Kōhei answers it.
- **Sitcom:** Rika's "'Ow!'" is the one sitcom beat. It's in her voice, but it stacks two Rika laughs in three lines. It's borderline, and acceptable.
- **Quippy:** the revision is less quippy than the original. No new button problem.

## 4. Beats, protected lines, continuity

- **Protected lines:** all present and exact, from "Cedar pollen." / "…It's April." and the bridge exchange to "You stopped for half a second. Don't." and the kept Chie texts 0262–0275.
- **Brief beats:** all intact (Lifesaver count, Nacchan on screen, the apron, the kids' plate, the two laughs, the walk-home realization, one-sided recognition, the bridge's half a date, the interlude's ending).
- **Continuity removals:** none came back (fourth dan, "Luck doesn't explain it," the house-in-the-dark simile, first-years present, sleeves, floor wax). Every mention of first-years is future or hypothetical.
- **Expressions:** the smirks at 0160 and 0231 reset in time, and the questions use `curious` where the outfit has it.
- **New facts:** "It's my favorite" (debana) is a small new preference; log it as [P]. "Don't draw that" implies Rika is sketching, which is consistent with her setup. No new props.
- **Risk:** 0284 is a Chie text. The brief keeps "the Chie texts" from the shipped pilot without listing them, and the rewrite log (B6) admits it couldn't tell whether 0284 is one. Editing an author-liked text on a guess isn't safe.

## 5. Read-aloud

- **0227** "She's holding her phone in both hands, like a cup filled too full." The simile was built for something being carried (the invitation). Hung on a phone, it's a mixed image and sounds written.
- **0201** "Late bloomers." Said alone, it doesn't sell the bluff.
- **0092 / 0207:** the missing replies read like a skipped line.
- Everything else reads naturally, including the new demo narration at 0143.

1. **0207 (Kōhei answers; goes where 0208 was).** New line after #c2_0207: `kohei(flustered): "…No." || TODO`. His literal straight-man answer is an accidental admission, so the pull shows and stays unnamed, and her laugh in 0209 now has something to laugh at.

4. **0227–0228.** `> She's holding her phone in both hands, like a cup filled too full.` → `> She's been carrying it since the dojo, like a cup filled too full.  #c2_0227` then `> So I say the only thing I've got.  #c2_0228`. "It wasn't." and "I don't know what to do with it." stay cut. Kōhei seeing her clearly is the pull. If "carrying it" still reads as telling, use the plain gesture without the simile ("She's turning her phone over in her hands.") rather than the mixed image.
5. **0284, restore exactly:** `@phone me yeah i'm good! made a friend. a real one i think. she gave me the tour on monday and showed me the vending machine that eats coins`. It's possibly a protected text, and it's charm either way. The echo of 0282 is a feature: what she privately treasures is the first thing she types.
6. **0201.** `"Late bloomers."` → `"Late bloomers. They're real. Look it up."` This brings back her doubling down without "It's a thing." The original Japanese (遅咲きのやつ。あるんだって。調べてみて。) already fits.
7. **0279, restore** (after #c2_0278): `> She didn't like not knowing how.  #c2_0279`. This is her reaction to the uncanny, and it gives the interlude the inner-life depth the brief asks for, in her competitive voice.
8. **0234.** `> I have to think about it.` → `> I think about it for longer than I'd like her to see.  #c2_0234`. One sentence, so the repetition is gone. With 0058 and 0075 cut, this is the only "than I'd like" left in the chapter.

Not fixes, just notes. "Yeah. You too." (0206) was built to answer "Good reflexes." It still works as a flub after a bare "thanks," and the Japanese pass should check that みずのも still makes sense. If a cold reader misses the zanshin irony in the match panel, 0145's second sentence is the thing to restore.

## 7. Verdict

**Ship the revision with fixes 1–8.** Don't go back to the original. Its Natsuki problems (the Nanoha deadpan, the lecture in the lesson, the sitcom tags after the catch) were real, and the revision solves them. The fixes add back about eight lines, almost all of them Kōhei's, plus a few small lines that carry her warmth.
