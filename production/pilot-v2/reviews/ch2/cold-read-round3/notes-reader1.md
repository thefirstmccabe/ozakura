**Kōhei and Natsuki:** Haven't met a Natsuki yet. No idea who she is.

**Strange or unexplained things:**

**What does Nanoha want?** Him, I think. More precisely, for him to notice her and to have a life. "So you'd hear yourself say it" was her pointing out that his schedule is all kendo, work and caregiving with no room for anything else. The Saturday shrine invite felt like a soft way of getting time with him.

**What does Natsuki want?** Unknown.

**What kind of story is this becoming?** A gentle slice-of-life school romance about a boy who has quietly become the parent of his household, with a supernatural or mystery layer around the old cherry tree and the fog. The opening makes me think something is coming that isn't just romance.

**What drove my guesses:**

**Strange or unexplained things:**

- Still unresolved from before: the cold open (fog, mirror, real katana, modern sleeve), the dream "since I was small," Mom, Dad, Akane's illness, the music room knocking, his off-center knot "for a year."

**What does Nanoha want?** Him, while being kind to everyone. And now (offstage) she's lost a little of her Saturday without knowing it yet.

**What does Natsuki want?** To stay somewhere long enough to reach the "second semester." To keep a person (she has exactly one, Chie). # Final answers

**When I became fairly sure (70%+) of anything:**
- That Nanoha is in love with Kōhei: part 2, "She went looking for mine before her own." (Part 3's "She did not let herself think about why it had needed deciding at all" took it to about 95%.)
- That this is a past-life or reincarnation story: part 4, "That someone was waiting for me. / That I kept them waiting far too long." I was sure once I noticed the dream's hilt matched the cold open.
- What Natsuki wants (belonging): part 3, "I've never stayed anywhere long enough to have one of those."
- That Natsuki has some real, non-ordinary connection to Kōhei: part 7, "Her eyes are wide and confused, like she doesn't know where it came from either."
- That Natsuki likes him romantically: part 8, "She's been carrying it since the dojo, like a cup filled too full."
- Never 70%+ on who he ends up with or who the dream woman is. I peaked at 60% (Natsuki, endgame, checkpoint 7) and 55% (Nanoha, dream, checkpoint 6). That ambiguity felt deliberate and mostly worked. By the end of Ch. 2 the scales tip a bit toward Natsuki.

**Hints that felt heavy-handed, too obvious or too coy:**
- *Heavy:* The Natsuki "I know you" signals pile up: the grin stutter, then "I feel like I've met him somewhere before. I just can't place it." the same day, then the tear, then the Chie text "i knew what he was going to do before he did it." Any two would do. The "met him somewhere before" line in particular says it outright, and it would land harder if she only *acted* it.
- *Heavy:* The tear is a well-worn trope, and the narration then explains it for me ("like she doesn't know where it came from either"). The image already shows the confusion.
- *Slightly over-explained:* "She went looking for mine before her own." The Fukuda-kun reveal had already made the point. Also "Her hands stay a second longer than they strictly need to" is a stock phrase.
- *Leaned on:* "It's April." Funny the first time. The Chie callback plus "why does everyone keep saying that" works as a button, but I'd stop there.
- *Just right:* "It's only trash bags." and the lozenge. Both trust the reader.
- *Too coy:* The cold open. Fog, a mirror, a hand on a katana, no words, and nothing pays it off in two chapters. It's only via the matching hilt in the dream that I could connect it to anything. One caption or line would help. The family situation (Dad, Mom behind the fusuma, Akane's illness) is also still entirely withheld. - *Possibly too subtle:* If the "Natsuki cuts the wrist / Nanoha binds it" parallel (kote strike vs. the dream's training cut; the lozenge vs. "Hurt again… Hold still") is intentional, it's lovely, but it got drowned out by the tear. I only caught it because I was being asked to guess.

**Flat lines (up to 8):**

2. Natsuki: "Allergies. It's allergies. Cedar pollen. Kanagawa's full of cedar, right?" The "Kanagawa" part sounds like the author telling me the prefecture, not like a flustered girl.

**Would I keep reading?** Yes. Hooks:
- Saturday at the shrine is now set up with all three of them, and Nanoha doesn't know yet.
- The tear and the "I knew what he'd do" mystery.
- Both girls are good people who like each other. That's rarer and more painful than a rival-villain setup.

What would make me stop:
