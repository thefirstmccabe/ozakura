- **Who does Kōhei end up with?** Nanoha, 55%. She's the only girl on the board so far (I'm told there are two, but Natsuki hasn't shown up). Childhood-friend-next-door is usually either the winner or the heartbreak, so 55 is generous.
- **Who do I want him to end up with?** Nanoha, 60%. She's funny. Hard to want anyone else when nobody else exists yet.
- **Woman in the dream:** not yet. (The opening fog/sword images aren't a woman, and I'm not sure they're a dream.)
- **Kōhei and Nanoha:** Lifelong neighbours who basically live in each other's kitchens. She knows his house better than he does (mugs cupboard, sticky drawer, window latch). She's clearly further along than he is: the collar, "hands stay a second longer," walking faster after he says he'll come Saturday. He notices her (petal on the braid, "it suits her") but files it away. Classic oblivious-ish protagonist, though he's more aware than most.
- **Kōhei and Natsuki:** not met yet.
- **Strange/unexplained:** The prologue: a foggy country crossroads with a traffic mirror, a man's hand gripping a real katana (not a kendo sword, it has a tsuba and scabbard), then a wall of fog rolling down a road like a wave, then the crossroads again. Ominous, supernatural or memory. Dad is gone (handwriting "last spring," never written over: dead or left, I'd guess dead). Mom is shut in her room, barely responds, so depression/grief. Akane has some condition (dizziness, "room starts tilting," clinic day on the 18th). The Ōzakura, an ancient cherry tree at the shrine, is name-dropped as the title, so it'll matter. Nanoha's grandpa runs the shrine.
- **What Natsuki wants:** unknown.
- **What Nanoha wants:** Him, I think, or at least for him to notice he's carrying the whole house. "So you'd hear yourself say it" is her pointing at how overbooked he is. She wants a slot in his schedule. The Saturday sweep sounds like a thing she engineered.
- **What kind of story:** Slice-of-life school romance with a grief/caretaker core, plus something uncanny tied to fog, a sword, and the big tree.
- **Drivers:** "Her hands stay a second longer than they strictly need to." / "We've always been keeping score. You're losing." / "All of it except the bottom line... in Dad's. He wrote it last spring. I've never written over it." / Akane: "…You always are." / the katana hand in fog.
- **Image note:** In the collar CG the river is on Kōhei's side, but the text says Nanoha walks on the river side. Small, and she's stepped in front of him, so it's defensible.
