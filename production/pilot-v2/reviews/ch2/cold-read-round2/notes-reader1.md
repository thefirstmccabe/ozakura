# Reader 1 notes

## Checkpoint 1 (after part 1)

- **Who Kōhei ends up with:** Nanoha, 60%. She's the only girl in the running so far (Akane is his little sister). Childhood-friend-next-door with a key to the kitchen is the classic "she's the one" setup, but also the classic "she loses to the new girl" setup, so I'm holding back.
- **Who I want him with:** Nanoha, 65%. She's funny ("You thought it in a short-person direction") and I already like her. Nobody to compare to yet.
- **Woman in the dream:** not yet. (The opening has fog, an empty road with a curve mirror, and a hand gripping a sword hilt, but no woman.)
- **Kōhei and Nanoha:** they're basically family. She walks in without knocking, knows which cupboard sticks, brings food from her mom. She clearly likes him (the collar, "hands stay a second longer than they strictly need to," walking faster after he says he'll come Saturday). He seems fond but oblivious, or busy pretending.
- **Strange/unexplained:** Mom stays shut in her room and barely answers. Dad's handwriting "last spring" and "I've never written over it" — Dad is dead or gone, probably about a year ago. Nanoha started bringing food "about a year now," so that lines up: something happened last spring. Akane has some illness (dizziness, "clinic day," "the room starts tilting"). The foggy opening with the sword hand is ominous; I don't know whose hand. The giant shrine cherry tree, the Ōzakura, is obviously going to matter (it's the title?).
- **What Nanoha wants:** him. Also to take care of him without him noticing, and to get him to admit he's overloaded ("So you'd hear yourself say it").
- **What Natsuki wants:** haven't met her.
- **What kind of story:** slice-of-life school romance with a grief undercurrent and something supernatural or dark lurking (fog, sword, sacred tree).
- **Drivers:** "Her hands stay a second longer than they strictly need to." / "She walks a little faster after that." / Dad's line on the whiteboard. / The sword hilt in the fog.
