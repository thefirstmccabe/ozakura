# Ch2 dialogue brief (for the diagnosis, rewrite and overcorrection check)

**Chapter:** Pilot v2 Ch2 "Kendo" (Tue Apr 8 – Wed Apr 9), source `production/pilot-v2/script/ch02.ozs`. Ch1 is approved; its script is `production/pilot-v2/script/ch01.ozs` (for voice reference only; don't revise it).

**What the chapter is for (approved outline, Oct 24):** Ch2 is Natsuki's chapter. The Ch1 cold reads had readers wanting Nanoha about 65% for structural reasons, so Ch2 has to make Natsuki as likable as Nanoha and pull the reader's wanting toward even. What she offers Kōhei (canon §6): with her he puts the load down; she's direct; she isn't careful with him. The romantic pull between them is visible (the catch, the bridge's half a date) but nobody names it. Nothing supernatural on screen; the tear stays unexplained. Under her cheer she's lonely, and unsettled by how well she read him; her interlude gives her inner life at the same depth as Nanoha's in Ch1.

**Scenes and the beats that must survive:**
1. **c2_tuesday:** the back-of-the-neck prickle in class ("she's always looking at the board"; the author kept it). Hoshino Kitchen on his shift: the manager's "Lifesaver!" count (one-note on purpose); the karaoke girls call her "Nacchan" on screen; she finds him in an apron, teases him, orders the kids' plate on purpose, stays until close talking about the town (never about him, never careful with him), and gets him to laugh twice. He notices only on the walk home how much he talked.
2. **c2_dojo:** Kirishima (male captain; deadpan that lands a beat late; "That was a joke." / nobody laughs). Takatsuki arrives through his clean, exact form; he never needles Kōhei about missed practices. Natsuki joins the club. "Old equipment. Well loved."
3. **c2_lesson:** Natsuki in teacher mode for Rika (glasses from the lost-and-found, a shinai pointer, the whiteboard): the targets, how a point scores, the calls, debana. Takatsuki as the model of correct form, Kōhei as the counterexample, which annoys him visibly. Every kendo term in Japanese with English.
4. **c2_match:** a manga insert (lettered panels). She's already there when he moves (debana-kote, "Kote ari!"); then his low, wrong-footed dō. The recognition is one-sided: she anticipates him, not the reverse.
5. **c2_debrief:** Kirishima: "You stopped for half a second. Don't." (keep exactly). Natsuki's joy at a clean point ("I know. That's why I'm so happy."). Takatsuki notices she read Kōhei better than he ever has.
6. **c2_mop:** he stays late without explaining why; the zōkin race ("Then I'm a spiritual first-year"; "I go after her. Of course I go after her."); she slips, he catches her (a short animation, then the CG); a tear she can't account for; "Cedar pollen." / "It's April." (keep exactly; the author likes it). No flash, no narrated feeling of recognition.
7. **c2_bridge:** their first walk alone. Keep "Saturday mornings. Club practice. You'll be there?" She counters: only if he tells her one thing he does for fun. "…Saturday afternoons are free." / "Doing what?" / "Carrying trash bags up at a shrine." / "Okay. That's my project, then." She's been wanting to photograph the big tree anyway. Half a date, unnamed. (She doesn't know Nanoha sweeps the shrine on Saturdays; he doesn't mention it.)
8. **c2_interlude:** Natsuki alone in the quiet apartment. "I'm amazing at first days. It's the second semesters I never get to." (keep the line). The Chie texts are kept from the shipped pilot (the author liked them), with "i knew what he was going to do before he did it. every time." She can't explain the tear or how she knew where he'd move. She likes Nanoha, and that's its own small worry. It ends on the cheerful text to Chie, then she photographs the cherry trees to send.
