# Ch2 "Kendo": dialogue diagnosis (draft `ch02.ozs`, 292 lines)

## 1. Blind voice test

**Natsuki lines that sound like Nanoha** (straight-faced logic, mock-formal self-assessment; compare Ch1's "I do supervise beautifully," "It's not luck. It's paperwork."):
- #c2_0071 "this is my sixth dojo, so my bow is excellent." This is Nanoha's mock-boast, and it's a self-label too.
- #c2_0092 "Everything's a little blurry, which is how I know they're working." Nanoha's inverted-logic theory.
- #c2_0104 "Exactly. Kendo is extremely polite." The "very X" deadpan tic, with "extremely" standing in for "very."
- #c2_0131 "your stance was perfect. Don't ruin it." Nanoha's private snark, or a generic sitcom comeback.
- Borderline: #c2_0100 "So be afraid." (fine on its own, but it's one more cap). #c2_0027 "He said they don't check." passes because it pulls Kōhei into the bit as an accomplice, which is outward.

**Lines anyone could say:** #c2_0110 "Totally calm about it." · #c2_0207 "Smooth." · #c2_0218 "That came out a lot sadder than it is." · Kōhei's #c2_0127 "I'm standing right here." and #c2_0208 "Shut up." · #c2_0039 "Everybody thinks that."

**Rika:** #c2_0088 "All I know is you guys yell" is the nearest she gets to dry understatement. It's acceptable as a setup, but her line should be the loud one.

**Clean:** Takatsuki (every line is his), Kirishima, the manager, Chie, and Kōhei's literal answers (#c2_0017, 0020, 0041, 0065, 0171, 0240). Kōhei's *narration* is wittier than his spoken straight man (#c2_0053, 0067, 0173). Ch1 does that sparingly; Ch2 does it at most scene ends.

**Charm parity.** Nanoha's Ch1 charm has four parts: committed running bits (the folder, the score, the face catalogue), precise domestic noticing, one sincere crack ("So you'd hear yourself say it."), and a care action (the collar). Natsuki has a match for each: the kids'-plate flag (ordered, photographed, pocketed, stood in the pen cup, the best running bit in the chapter), the apron knot, "So I have proof I was there," and putting the chairs up without a word. In the restaurant, the bridge and the interlude she is as charming as Nanoha. She loses ground in two places. In the lesson she becomes a stand-up with Nanoha's timing, and she makes statements instead of asking. After the catch she gets stock sitcom tags. Her noticing also disappears after Tuesday: the only physical detail she reads later is #c2_0125, and that's delivered as a lecture.

## 3. Scene-level problems

**Buttons.** Almost every micro-exchange ends on a cap: the apron (0021), the taiyaki (0043), the chairs (0050, then 0052, then 0053: three buttons in a row), the knot (0066 + 0067), four lesson caps (0100, 0104, 0110, 0114), Takatsuki (0131, then Kirishima tops it), the debrief (0163, 0173), the catch (0207/0208) and the bridge (0218). The narrated scene-enders 0058, 0173 and 0251 are buttons. Kirishima's endings (0081–0082, 0134) are his designed mechanism; keep them. 0210 "We finish the floor. We don't race." is the model the other scene ends should follow.

**Feelings stated:** 0045, 0058, 0075, 0163, 0227–0228, 0251, 0279. Most of them are Kōhei vouching for a beat the reader has already felt.

**Humor mechanisms:** Kōhei, Takatsuki, Kirishima, the manager and Rika are right. Natsuki drifts in 0071 and in the lesson. Rika's specific obsession is set up and then dropped: the art-club "people in motion" sketchbook vanishes after 0087, and it's the obvious payoff (she sketches the stance comparison or the match).

**Narration vouching:** 0013, 0053, 0145, 0163, 0165, 0173, 0217, 0227. 0257 pre-states the dialogue and 0284 repeats 0282.

**Tics (§3b):**
- "says it / the way": 0002 (fine as the chapter's one), 0165 (both), 0217.
- "than I'd like/want": 0058, 0075, 0234.
- "I don't know why/what": 0045, 0123, 0228. Keep 0123; it's funny.
- "never once": 0019, 0069, 0166. Keep 0166.
- "I don't think": 0147, 0187.
- "That's not why/how—": 0105, 0181.
- "very" family: 0104.
- Face-reading: **none. Pass.**
- Americanisms: 0021, 0067, 0159, 0201, 0220.
- Simile load is heavy: Takatsuki alone gets four (0061 diagram, 0118 door, 0129 answer key, 0165 scorecard). Keep the diagram and the door.

**Restaurant length:** 58 lines, the longest scene (about 20% of the chapter), longer than the lesson (53) and the bridge (41). That isn't "short." The required beats all work; the excess is explaining narration (0008, 0013, 0045, 0053, 0058) plus the dish-pit beat (0031–0032). Those cuts bring it to about 50 lines without losing a beat.

**Lesson: fun or lecture?** The frame is fun: the borrowed glasses, the big-headed stick figure, the "Fu-ji-sa-wa!" chant, "visual aid," "From memory." The middle (0101–0114) is a lecture. It runs four definitions in a row (0102, 0106, 0108, 0112) on a fixed loop: Rika asks, Natsuki defines, Rika reframes, Natsuki caps. Rika's reframes (0103, 0109) are the real laughs, and Natsuki's caps step on them. Natsuki also stops asking questions, which is her own mechanism.

## 4. The eight highest-value fixes (ranked)

1. **Take Nanoha's deadpan out of Natsuki** (0071, 0092, 0104, 0131; cut 0110). Give each beat her own move: a question, a challenge, or a poke at a physical detail.
2. **Clean up the catch aftermath.** Replace "It's a thing. Look it up." / "Smooth." / "Shut up." (0201, 0207–0208) with Natsuki closing on her own laugh or a poke. Trim the stock lines around the catch (0187, 0190, 0194's first two fragments).
3. **Restore Tuesday's walk-home realization and shorten the scene.** Cut 0045 and 0058, trim 0008, 0013, 0053 and the dish-pit beat, and end on 0057.
4. **Turn the lesson from glossary into game.** Natsuki quizzes Rika instead of defining, demonstrates the shout instead of describing it (and names ki-ken-tai/kiai, glossed, in 0102), splits 0108, lets Rika's reframes be the buttons, and pays off the sketchbook.
5. **Cut the narrated buttons and vouching** in the dojo, match and debrief (0067, 0145's second sentence, 0163, 0165, 0173) so "Yes." and the laughing panel land without commentary.
6. **Keep the bridge's subtext under the surface.** Cut 0218's self-commentary, 0217's "She says it," 0227–0228 and 0234's repeat, and trim 0251 to "It's only trash bags."
7. **Give Natsuki her noticing back in the dojo half.** Add one physical-detail poke at Kōhei after the match or at the mop, and make 0160 a question as the Japanese has it, so her mechanism runs through the whole chapter and not just Tuesday.
8. **Run a tic sweep.** Target "than I'd like/want," "I don't know why/what," "never once," the Takatsuki simile pile, and the Americanisms (0021, 0159, 0220). In the interlude, cut 0257's first-week sentence and the duplicate vending machine in 0284.
