## 2. A writing rule (author): every callback carries its own context
Readers won't come to a chapter with perfect memory of the last one. A callback to an earlier chapter has to land cold, so restate what it calls back to in the line itself. For example: "On the bridge, I told you I had a project." / "And you wouldn't say what." The bare version was "'What is?' And I didn't answer." For Story's speech and outline notes and Planning's §9 gates (the dialogue brief and the cold read could check it). What Ch3 restated is listed in the punch list record.
