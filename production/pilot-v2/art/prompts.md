## cg_collar (river walk)
- **Current version (Oct 17, after the fifth check):** prompting alone kept getting her scale and the location wrong, so this one starts from a **layout sketch** (`production/pipeline/vn/cg_layout.py` → `production/pilot-v2/art/layout/cg_collar_layout.jpg`): a crop of `img/bg/bg_river_morning.webp` with Kōhei (`inventory/full-kohei.png`, mirrored) and Nanoha (`img/ch/nanoha_school_smile.webp`, lifted 2 in for her toes) pasted at their true heights (32 px per inch, frame bottom 24 in above the ground). Nanoha now stands on the right facing left, so her braid and clip (her left side) face the camera.
- `fal-ai/nano-banana-pro/edit`, 2K, 16:9. Refs: the layout, `img/ch/nanoha_school_smile.webp`, `v2/img/face/kohei_smile.webp`. 2 takes; **picked seed 62**. Both takes moved the fence between them, so one edit moved it behind him ("Move the wooden fence to the LEFT of the boy, so it runs behind him along the top of the grassy embankment, between him and the river. Both of them now stand together on the same petal-strewn path …").
- Prompt (key lines): "Repaint image 1 into one finished, seamless illustration in the same style. Image 1 is a layout: keep the location exactly as it is … keep both characters exactly where they stand and exactly this size. Do not shrink or enlarge either of them … Change only their poses: they turn to face each other … Kohei … turns right toward her, head tilted down, looking down at her … Nanoha … turns left toward him and stands up on her toes, both arms raised and bent upward, her hands at his neck folding his white shirt collar down … Her thick braid and her hair clip are on her left side, which now faces the viewer …" plus the uniform, style and color lines.
- **Result:** both on the path, the fence between him and the river, her head at his chin, her head about 0.8× his (real proportions would be nearer 0.95), braid and clip on the visible side, arms raised.
- **For later two-character CGs:** start from a layout sketch at true heights; expect the model to drift on positions anyway and plan one edit pass; choose the side each character faces from their signature features.
