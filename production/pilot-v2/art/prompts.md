Medium two-shot from the side at a three-quarter angle, from the waist up, both faces clearly visible, the river and the cherry trees behind them, a few petals drifting in the air and one petal resting on her hair clip. School uniforms: navy blazers with gold buttons, white shirts, a burgundy tie on him, a burgundy ribbon on her, charcoal plaid pleated skirt.
