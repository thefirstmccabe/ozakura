## cg_collar (river walk)
- **Current version (Oct 17, fourth review):** the third-review version drew Nanoha's whole figure smaller to meet "head at his chin" (her head about 0.64× his, so she read younger), stood them off the path on the river side of the fence, and lost her braid. Redrawn.
- `fal-ai/nano-banana-pro/edit`, 2K, 16:9. Refs: `img/ch/nanoha_school_smile.webp`, `production/refs/inventory/full-kohei.png`, `img/bg/bg_river_morning.webp`, `v2/img/face/kohei_smile.webp`, `production/refs/handoff/Nanoha-School-Braid.png` (braid only, not its proportions), `production/refs/lineup/lineup-1-school.png` (relative sizes). 2 takes; **picked seed 52** (her single thick braid, heads nearly the same size, both on the path with the fence between her and the river, him looking down at her; seed 51 gave her loose waves and a thin braid). It put the braid and clip on her near side, so one edit moved the braid over the front of her far (left) shoulder, with its tail showing in front of her blazer, and removed the clip from the near side.
- Prompt (key lines): "Keep image 3's layout exactly … Both of them stand ON THE PATH, with the wooden fence running behind Nanoha, between her and the river … Her braid is on her LEFT side and hangs forward over the front of her left shoulder, so in this side view its tail shows in front of her chest near her ribbon, partly behind her raised arm; her hair clip is on her left side, hidden on the far side of her head … his head tilted down, looking down at her face … use image 6 (the cast lineup) for their relative sizes. She is a petite 16-year-old girl, 4 feet 11 inches; he is 5 feet 9 inches. Her head is nearly as big as his … Up on her toes, the top of her head reaches just under his chin. She stretches up, arms raised …" plus the uniform, style and "FULL NATURAL COLOR" lines used throughout.
- **Lessons for later CGs:** (1) say that heads stay nearly the same size and the shorter character's body is shorter, and pass the cast lineup, or the model shrinks her whole figure; (2) a side view shows only one side of a design, so plan which side of Nanoha (braid and clip on her left) faces the camera, and expect an edit pass if it lands on the wrong side.
