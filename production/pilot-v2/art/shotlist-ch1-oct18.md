## New
1. **Cold open (P.0), three CGs** in misty early-morning light. The treatment is distinct from both era palettes [P]: cool gray-green, low contrast, no sun disc.
   - `cg_cold_mirror`: a convex traffic mirror on a pole at a bend in a narrow modern road between hedges and utility poles, swallowed by mist. Nobody in frame, or only a blur in the mirror.
   - `cg_cold_hand`: one male hand in a modern dark jacket sleeve closing on the grip of a sword. Only the wrapped grip and guard show, no blade. Tight framing; mist behind; no face, no other figure.
   - `cg_cold_mist`: the road beyond the bend, only mist, moving as if something huge is inside it. Nothing visible in it.
   - The script (after the continuity check) runs mirror → hand → mist → **the same mirror image again**, so the landmark passes a second time, then cuts to black.
   - **Living still** on `cg_cold_mist`: the mist rolling (Kling v3 Pro, start = end frame).
   - **Never:** faces, eyes, a mask or a wolf shape, the tree, the boulder, a kimono, petals or visible breath.
2. **Audio [P]:** a wind bed under the cold open (one generated loop, about $0.10), or silence. The outline allows either.
