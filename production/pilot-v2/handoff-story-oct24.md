The Ch2–4 outline and the chapter map are yours to update; the outline's 1.5 line ("the three first-year confessions come out, and so does 'I don't have time'") is now stale.

## 2. Kōhei and the sword (a light past-life hint, now in Ch1)
The author: "this is where we get some insight into Kohei. He talks about always feeling comfortable with a sword in his hand." Canon §2 already says he loves the feel of a sword, from his past life, and finds peace in it.
