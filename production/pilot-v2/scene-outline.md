## Cold open (rewritten Nov 21, staging corrected Nov 25, Akane in the sibling's place Nov 25; canon §1; built after Ch4)

Everything else below is **[P]** staging.
- **Who and when:** a cold January dusk; his modern coat, the traffic mirror and his kendo bag keep it plainly the present. Takatsuki walks Akane home along an ordinary road, his kendo bag on his shoulder; why he's the one walking her is Ch37's to show [P in the scene list]. **His point of view:** his face window and his full-name tag ("Takatsuki Shō") from the first line, so nobody takes him for the lead. She calls him "Takatsuki-senpai"; he calls her "Akane-san" (style guide §3c). No tree, no boulder, no shrine.
- **The road turns into woods:** the houses thin out where they shouldn't; a convex traffic mirror at a bend comes around again [P]. He counts it out in his formal way ("The same mirror. That is the third time.") and keeps his voice even for her. The mist comes up; behind them, footsteps that stop when they stop.
- **The shinai:** he puts her behind him ("Stay behind me.") and draws his shinai from the bag. In the mist, the eyes. She goes down [P: her legs give out after too long on her feet, one of her spells, more than fright]. Nothing happens to her: the eyes hold where they are. **Since Nov 25 (author) this wolf doesn't follow the legend:** the reader later hears that a fall brings the wolf (Ch3's "just stopping for a rest," Ch16's kaidan), and Ch37 closes that on screen when Takatsuki registers it didn't go the way the story says (canon §6).
- **The rescue begins:** flashlight beams through the trees and voices calling "Takatsuki!" Two or three voices from the January group, Kōhei's among them, with no name tags and no faces. Cut to black on the light reaching them, before anyone is seen and before how they get out. (Ch37, author Nov 25: Nanoha insists they go right now, and she and Kōhei come on her dad's motorcycle; only Nanoha can get people out, Working. If the cold open should carry an engine under the voices is [P] for the scene list.)
- **Shown and never shown:** the eyes in the mist, and nothing more of the beast (no mask or wolf shape; Ch5's eyes dream echoes this). Never shown: anything that reads as the past life, and who stands next to whom.

### Cold open + Prologue + Ch1
- **Know:** something dangerous waits in this story. **Nov 21, with Akane since Nov 25:** in some winter to come, Takatsuki and Akane are caught where their road home turns into woods, with something's eyes in the mist, and other people come for them. Kōhei carries the house (Dad died last spring, Mom stays behind the fusuma, Akane is unwell) and won't admit he's tired. Nanoha loves him and hides it; she's part of his home; he notices more than he lets himself think about. His recurring dream is of another life: a swordsman, a woman binding his cut wrist, "You're late," a branch and petals overhead, a river. The Ōzakura is a huge old cherry tree on the hill. He and Natsuki reacted to each other on sight, and neither explains it.
- **Suspect:** the dream is a past life with a wife or lover; one of the two girls is tied to it, and it could be either. The Ōzakura may be the dream's tree.
- **Not yet:** what's in the mist beyond its eyes; how they get out (author, Nov 21; readers may guess who came); whether either girl remembers anything; how that life ended; that it ended in winter.
- **Direction for Ch1 (not a gate; the window is Ch1–4):** don't leave Ch1 tilted toward Nanoha as the dream woman, since Ch3–4 tilt that way on their own. Every reader expects danger beyond the romance; all read the dream as a past life with a wife or lover; none says the premise was stated outright.

**Decisions for the Ch1 revision:**

2. **The wrist simile becomes a plain action [P].** At lunch Natsuki writes the kanji for Wednesday (水) on the inside of her wrist in ballpoint, small, under her cuff: a habit from six schools' worth of new timetables. Plain description, no simile, no comment.
