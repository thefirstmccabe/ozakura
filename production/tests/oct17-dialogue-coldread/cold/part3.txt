She walks to the front of a room full of strangers like she's walking onto a court she's already won on.
Copper hair, cut neat at her shoulders and tucked behind one ear. Her uniform looks like it was ironed by someone who meant it.
Natsuki: "I'm Mizuno Natsuki. It's nice to meet you all."
Natsuki: "My dad's job moves us around a lot, so I've been the new girl…"

Natsuki: "…six times now? So I'm basically a professional. Please don't go easy on me."
A ripple of laughter. Someone in the front row actually claps.
Natsuki: "I do kendo, and I'm looking for a dojo that'll put up with me. I also really love horror movies, so if anyone wants to watch one with me, I'll be grateful forever."
Her eyes go along the rows while she says it. Then they get to me.

Natsuki: "Please take care of me."
She bows, and the class gives her the friendly kind of applause.
I realize I've been holding my pen hard enough to hurt.

I keep my eyes on the board for the entire first period.
I don't hear a single word of it.

[Background: classroom, daytime]
By lunch, Mizuno Natsuki has learned the names of half the class, answered "Sendai, most recently" about twenty times, been invited to two clubs, and agreed to go to karaoke with a group of girls whose names I don't think I've ever said out loud.
I'm impressed. I'm also slightly exhausted just watching.
I've barely gotten the lid off my lunch when a chair scrapes behind me.
[Natsuki appears on screen]
Natsuki: "So. You're Fujisawa."
Kōhei: "That's what the attendance sheet says."
Natsuki: "The guy who turned down three confessions last year."
I put my chopsticks down.
Kōhei: "Is that in the welcome packet?"
Natsuki: "Someone told me before I'd even put my shoes in my locker. I'm not kidding. The girl by the door was very passionate about it."
Kōhei: "Great."
Natsuki: "She said you told every one of them you didn't have time for a girlfriend. Is that true?"
Kōhei: "It's true that I don't have time."
Natsuki: "Huh."
She props her chin on her hand and looks at me like she's reading a scorecard.
Natsuki: "That's either really honest or really sad. I can't decide."
Kōhei: "Let me know when you do."
Natsuki: "I will. I'm very decisive. Usually."
Something about the way she says it makes me laugh.
Natsuki: "Okay, you're fun. So what are you, shodan (first dan)?"
Kōhei: "We'll find out on the floor."
Natsuki: "Oh, he's cocky. Good. I hate practicing with polite people."
Kōhei: "Club starts Wednesday."
Natsuki: "Wednesday."
She repeats it like she's writing it on the inside of her wrist.
[Nanoha appears on screen]
Nanoha: "Excuse me. Mizuno-san?"
Nanoha's standing by my desk with her lunch held in front of her in both hands, the way she holds things when she's about to do something brave.
Natsuki: "Oh—hi. You're…"
Nanoha: "Tachibana Nanoha. I sit two rows over. I was the class representative's assistant last year, so I still own a clipboard."
Nanoha: "If you'd like, I could show you around after school? The library, the music rooms. Which shoe lockers stick. Which vending machine eats coins. That kind of thing."
For a second Mizuno doesn't say anything. She looks almost startled, like she isn't used to being the one who gets asked.
Natsuki: "…Really? You'd do that?"
Nanoha: "It's not a big tour. The vending machine part is the most important."
Natsuki: "I would love that. Really. Thank you."
Natsuki: "Oh, and call me Natsuki. Is that okay? People tell me I'm pushy."
Nanoha: "…Natsuki-san."
Natsuki: "Eh, 'san'? We're the same age!"
Nanoha: "I'm working up to it. I need time to adjust."
Natsuki: "Okay, okay. Then I'll just call you Nanoha. Is that all right? I'm going to do it anyway."
Nanoha: "That's—yes. That's fine."
They both laugh, and something about the two of them laughing in the window light, over the top of my head, makes me feel weirdly like furniture.
I eat my rice. It's very good rice. I made it myself.
