# Reader 2 cold-read notes

## Checkpoint 1 (after part1.txt)

1. **Who is the dream woman?** No characters met yet. My guess: a woman from a past life (the CG looks like samurai-era Japan), who has been reborn as someone the narrator is about to meet. The chapter title "The House Next Door" makes me bet she's the girl next door. Confidence it's "the neighbor girl, reincarnated": ~40%. Confidence it's a past-life figure of some kind (not just a symbolic dream): ~75%.

2. **Relationship?** Wife, most likely. He's coming *home* ("held it closed the whole way home"), she's there waiting, she scolds him for being late, and "Hurt again" says this happens a lot. That reads domestic to me, like a wife who patches up her husband. Wife 45%, lover/fiancée 20%, sister 15%, mother 10%, other 10%.

3. **What's going on?** A reincarnation romance. The narrator (presumably a modern high-schooler) has dreamed since childhood about a past life as a swordsman, and a woman who waited for him and whom he "kept waiting far too long." So he probably died or left and didn't come back. I expect he'll meet her reincarnation (the neighbor?) and the story will be about recognizing her. School romance with a fated-lovers/past-life layer, maybe bittersweet.

4. **What drove it:**
   - CG: a large, rough, veined adult man's forearm (not a teenager's), a katana hilt at the left, a woman in a striped kimono and obi, cherry blossoms. That screams past life / Edo-period swordsman.
   - "There's a cut on the inside of it from training, and I've held it closed the whole way home."
   - "You're late." / "She says it like she's been waiting a very long time." This works on two levels, late home tonight and late across lifetimes.
   - "Hurt again. Give me your hand… Hold still." Familiar and a bit bossy, intimate.
   - "That I kept them waiting far too long." Guilt; he didn't come back?
   - Chapter title: "The House Next Door."
   - Small detail: she binds the wrist "like the knot matters more than anything else in the world." I suspect the cloth/knot will be a recurring object (a ribbon? a hair tie?) that identifies her in the present.

## Checkpoint 2 (after part2.txt)

1. **Who is the dream woman?** Nanoha, the childhood friend next door, in a past life. Confidence: 50%. Runner-up: the unnamed transfer student "from somewhere far," 25%. She's the classic genre rival for the "destined" slot, and the story planting her this early makes me suspicious. ~15% it's something tied to the Ōzakura tree (a tree spirit or a woman bound to that place) who isn't any girl we've met. ~10% everything else (Akane, Mom, Rika: unlikely).

2. **Relationship?** Past-life wife, or a lover who was as good as one: 55%. In the present she's a childhood friend who's clearly in love with him while he's oblivious, which mirrors "she's been waiting a very long time." Lover/betrothed 20%, sibling 10%, other 15%. I've dropped "mother" a lot. The dream framing is romantic and domestic, and his actual mother is a separate thread.

3. **What's going on?** Two layers now.
   - Present: Kōhei is a second-year who's holding his household together. Dad is gone as of "last spring" (dead, I think, since the handwriting he's never written over feels like grief), Mom shuts herself in her room (depression after the loss?), and his little sister Akane has some chronic condition (dizziness, a clinic day). He cooks, does kendo and works shifts at "Hoshino Kitchen" (likely Nanoha's family restaurant?). He's over-scheduled on purpose so he doesn't have to feel anything. Nanoha has quietly been propping the family up for a year (the "Mom made too much" food).
   - Mystery: the recurring past-life dream about a swordsman and a woman under cherry blossoms by a river, and the big tree "the Ōzakura" at Nanoha's grandpa's place (a shrine?). I expect the Ōzakura to be where the past-life story happened.
   - Story type: a slice-of-life school romance with a reincarnation/fate hook, plus a grief/family-healing drama underneath. It's set up as a childhood friend vs. mysterious transfer student triangle, which makes me worry the childhood friend loses, as she often does in these.

4. **What drove it:**

   - "The back door slides open. No knock. There's never a knock." Chapter title "The House Next Door," so she's the neighbor.
   - "Nanoha: 'I know.' / 'Then why'd you ask?' / 'So you'd hear yourself say it.'" Scolding-but-caring, close to the dream's "Hurt again."
   - She found his name before her own. She's been prioritizing him forever ("waiting a very long time").
   - Petal landing on her hair clip, "It suits her." Petals tie her visually to the dream.
   - "Kendo: Monday, Wednesday..." links to the dream's "cut on the inside of it from training." Kōhei is the swordsman reborn.
   - Against Nanoha: it's almost too neat for chapter 1, and "Two-B is getting a transfer student! A girl! From somewhere! Far!" is a classic setup for the "real" fated girl. Also the dream woman's hands in the CG look slender and adult; can't tell more.
   - Rika's "Hm. Hmmm." about Kōhei being in the same class tells me everyone except Kōhei knows Nanoha likes him.

I now expect a reincarnation romance where *who* the dream woman was is the central question, and the answer will hurt one of the two girls.    - "Left palm. You've got the callus right where the tsuka (hilt) sits." She's focused on his *hands*, as the dream woman was. "Takes one to know one."
   - "I do kendo." Swordsmanship links her to the dream's katana.

   - "makes me feel weirdly like furniture" suggests the two girls will build a friendship around him. Their relationship may matter more than either one's relationship with him.

## Checkpoint 4 (after part4.txt, end of chapter)

1. **Who is the dream woman?** Back to Nanoha, narrowly. Nanoha 55%, Natsuki 35%, someone not yet met 10%. All the *supernatural* signals point at Natsuki (falter, wrist, now "met before"). All the *character* signals point at Nanoha: the dream woman's voice (scolding while laughing, "Hurt again… Hold still") is exactly Nanoha's register, the dream woman is a caretaker at home, not a fighter, and "waiting a very long time" is literally Nanoha's situation. My read now: Natsuki remembers *something*, but maybe she was a different person in the past life (a rival or comrade swordsman? the one who got him killed?), and the woman who waited was Nanoha. 2. **Relationship?** Past-life wife: 50%. Lover/betrothed who waited for him to come home and he never did: 25%. Something else (sibling, etc.): 25%. I'm fairly sure the dream is about a man who didn't come back to someone. "Kept them waiting far too long" now also reads as the present: he's kept Nanoha waiting for sixteen years.

3. **What's going on?** A reincarnation love story disguised as a school-romance triangle. Kōhei, a reborn swordsman, has fragmentary dreams. Natsuki, the swordswoman transfer student, feels she's met him before, so she carries some past-life memory too. Nanoha, the childhood friend who knows every sticking drawer, is quietly in love and has just "decided" to like her rival so she won't have to compete. Underneath is the grief plot (Dad dead or gone a year ago, Mom withdrawn, Akane ill, Kōhei over-functioning). I expect the Ōzakura tree on Saturday to trigger the next dream piece. Genre: bittersweet romance drama with a light supernatural mystery. Probably heading for a "which girl is the one from the dream" reveal that'll break someone's heart, maybe with the twist that the obvious fated girl isn't the dream girl.

   - "You have several. I've catalogued them." She's studied him for years, like a wife would.
   - Window across a gap "so narrow you could pass a book through it if you leaned." / "I stand there for a while longer than the garbage requires." Ending on Nanoha says the emotional center of the story is her, at least for now.
   - Dad's handwriting on the calendar shows up again ("tonight I went with the handwriting"). Quietly devastating, and it confirms grief as the undercurrent.
