[Interlude: Nanoha, told in third person]
[Background: classroom, evening]
Natsuki noticed everything.
That was the first thing Nanoha learned on the tour. Natsuki noticed that the stairs by the art room squeaked on the fourth step and the eleventh. She noticed the faded rectangle on the wall where a trophy case used to be. She noticed that the third-floor girls' bathroom had a window that looked straight at the mountains, and she said "Oh," very quietly, like she'd been handed something.
They ended the tour in their own empty classroom, the evening sun coming in sideways and turning every desk gold. Natsuki dropped into the chair in front of her own, turned it around, and sat backward on it with her arms folded over the backrest.
[Natsuki appears on screen]
Natsuki: "This school's nice. It feels… lived in. Does that make sense?"
Nanoha: "It's very old. The heating sounds like a ghost."
Natsuki: "Perfect. I love it already."
Natsuki: "So. You and Fujisawa."
Nanoha: "Me and Kōhei?"
Natsuki: "You live next door, right? Somebody said."
Nanoha: "Since we were born."
Natsuki: "No way. So you're real childhood friends."
Nanoha: "Real childhood friends."
Nanoha said it as lightly as she knew how. She'd had practice.
Natsuki: "That's really nice."
Natsuki: "I've never stayed anywhere long enough to have one of those. You know? Someone who knows which drawer sticks in your kitchen."
Nanoha didn't say that Kōhei's kitchen drawer stuck. She didn't say she'd known it since she was six.
Natsuki: "Hey. This is going to sound weird."
Nanoha: "I like weird."
Natsuki: "Does he ever seem like… someone you've met before?"
Nanoha: "…"
Nanoha: "I have met him before. Every day. For sixteen years."
Natsuki: "Okay, fair! Fair. Forget it. It's the jet lag talking."
Nanoha: "You moved from Sendai. That's barely three hours by train."
Natsuki: "Very intense jet lag."
Natsuki laughed first. The moment closed up, and it was fine.
It was fine.
Natsuki: "Thanks for today, Nanoha. Seriously. First days are usually…"
She didn't finish. She just smiled, and it was a different smile from the one she'd used in front of the class. Smaller. It looked like it cost her something.
Natsuki: "Anyway. Thanks."
Somewhere between the library and the gym, Nanoha had decided that she liked Mizuno Natsuki.
She'd decided it firmly, the way you shut a window against the wind.
She did not let herself think about why it had needed deciding at all.

[Background: two houses side by side at night]
I'm taking the burnables out to the corner. Pickup is Tuesdays and Fridays now. Dad's handwriting on the calendar still says Monday, and tonight I went with the handwriting.
That's when Nanoha comes up the street with her school bag over her shoulder.
[Nanoha appears on screen]
Nanoha: "You're taking out the trash on the wrong day."
Kōhei: "I'm moving it closer to the right day. How was the tour?"
Nanoha: "Good. She noticed the squeaky stairs. Both of them."
Kōhei: "Nobody notices the eleventh step."
Nanoha: "She did."
Nanoha: "She's nice, Kōhei. I like her."
She says it a little too clearly, the way you say something so it stays said.
Nanoha: "What did you think of her?"
Kōhei: "She seems fine."
Nanoha: "You smiled."
Kōhei: "When?"
Nanoha: "When she said she was looking for a dojo that would put up with her. You did your—"
She drops her chin and narrows her eyes and does a frankly offensive impression of what I can only assume is my face.
Kōhei: "I don't have a face."
Nanoha: "You have several. I've catalogued them. That one's number four. 'Somebody wants to fight me.'"
Kōhei: "What's number one?"
Nanoha: "Secret."
She walks the ten feet to her own front door. Pauses with her hand on the latch.
Nanoha: "Good night, Kō-chan."
Kōhei: "Night."
The door closes.
[Background: the two houses at night; an upstairs window across the gap lights up]
A moment later her upstairs light comes on, in the window that faces mine across a gap so narrow you could pass a book through it if you leaned.
I stand there for a while longer than the garbage requires.
[Background: black screen]
[End of Chapter 1]
