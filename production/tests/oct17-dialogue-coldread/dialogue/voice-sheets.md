# Ōzakura: how each character talks (PROVISIONAL, Claude, Oct 17)

Built from canon §8 personalities. These are speech rules, not personality summaries. The core problem to avoid: every character sharing one writer's crisp, aphoristic wit. Each character gets a DIFFERENT humor mechanism, rhythm and way of dodging feelings.

General rules for all dialogue
- Translated-manga feel: natural modern English, contractions, honorifics kept (-san, -kun, -chan, senpai, Onii-chan).
- Not every exchange ends on a button or punchline. Some lines just trail off, get interrupted, get ignored or miss.
- People rarely say what they feel. They deflect, change the subject, act, or say something adjacent.
- Real talk is uneven: someone answers the wrong question, someone repeats a word, someone talks over someone.
- Restraint (Blue Box): quiet moments stay quiet; nobody gets wittier just to be lively.
- No line explains its own joke. No character narrates their own feelings in therapy language.
- No characters comparing their situation to manga.

## Kōhei (narrator; 16; head of a struggling household)
- **Rhythm:** short. One sentence, often a fragment. Rarely more than two in a row. Long speeches only under real pressure.
- **Humor mechanism:** the straight man. Literal answers, understatement, mock-seriousness. He plays along with other people's bits rather than topping them. He doesn't coin polished one-liners.
- **Register:** plain, a little rough, practical. Male casual Japanese (ore, -daro) under the English.
- **Dodges feelings by:** going practical (schedules, logistics, food), "It's fine," or changing the subject. Sincere flirtation flusters him: he goes shorter, not cleverer.
- **Tells:** competitive edge when challenged (gets terse and a little cocky). Checks on Akane by looking, not asking twice.
- **Never:** quips back with equal polish to a flirt; speeches about himself; admitting he's tired.

## Nanoha (16; childhood friend; two registers)
- **Private (Kōhei, Akane, family):** deadpan absurdist. Builds elaborate, committed theories and bits delivered completely straight (the class-assignment conspiracy). Keeps score. Precise vocabulary; she's very smart.
- **Public / strangers:** gentle, polite, reserved, a little formal. Shorter lines, small hesitations ("Um—", "If you'd like…"). Not quippy with someone she's just met; the deadpan leaks out only in small, dry touches.
- **Humor mechanism:** the committed bit (private); a single dry understatement (public).
- **Dodges feelings by:** hiding inside the bit, a quiet "Mm," or a very specific practical detail (which drawer sticks). Never states what she wants.
- **Tells:** remembers small details and follows up. Brave moments look like formality.
- **Never:** snark, teasing a stranger, loud enthusiasm, saying "I like you" plainly about Kōhei.

## Natsuki (16; transfer student; warm, confident, charismatic)
- **Rhythm:** easy, fast, conversational. Questions. "Hey." "So." Uses people's names early.
- **Humor mechanism:** playful challenge and provocation; teasing by observation (she notices physical details: calluses, posture, which stair squeaks). Competitive banter, pokes to see how you'll react.
- **Register:** casual, friendly, a little bold; never loud or boisterous.
- **Dodges feelings by:** getting MORE entertaining when hurt or afraid; making big invitations sound casual; a laugh to close a moment.
- **Tells:** when sincere or caught off guard she goes short and quiet, and the performance drops for a beat.
- **Distinct from Nanoha:** Natsuki's humor goes outward (pokes, challenges, asks). Nanoha's goes inward (deadpan, elaborate, straight-faced). Natsuki asks; Nanoha states.
- **Never:** coy or demure as a default; mean; flirting in a way that reads as practiced lines.

## Akane (14–15; Kōhei's frail little sister)
- **Rhythm:** sing-song, a little younger. Exaggeration for effect.
- **Humor mechanism:** sibling teasing; resisting being managed by pouting and cheerful defiance ("Onii-chan, you're hovering again").
- **Tells:** sees through Kōhei; a quiet sincere line under the teasing.
- **Never:** sniping or mean; adult-sounding wit.

## Rika (16; Nanoha's best friend; art club)
- **Rhythm:** exclamation points, run-ons, sudden topic jumps. Nicknames ("Nano-chan").
- **Humor mechanism:** enthusiasm and non-sequitur; scheming she thinks is subtle.
- **Never:** dry or understated.

## Ryōsuke (Tokiwa; 16; Kōhei's best friend; glasses)
- **Rhythm:** measured, complete sentences, structured arguments.
- **Humor mechanism:** pedantry about trivia, argued with total seriousness; dry and selective.
- **Dodges feelings by:** retreating into trivia; avoids personal embarrassment.
- **Never:** emotional speeches; enthusiasm.
