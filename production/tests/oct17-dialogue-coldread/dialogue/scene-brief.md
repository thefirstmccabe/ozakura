# Scene brief: Ch1 "Lunch with Mizuno" (c1_lunch)

**Format:** `>` lines are Kōhei's first-person present-tense narration (unvoiced). `who<expr>: "line"` is spoken (voiced in Japanese; English shown). `kohei(expr):` is Kōhei speaking with his face window. `@` lines are staging; keep them unless a change needs one.

**Context (what the reader already knows at this point):**
- Prologue: a recurring dream of a woman binding a cut on Kōhei's wrist ("You're late." / "Hurt again. Give me your hand… Hold still.").
- Morning: Kōhei runs the household (makes two lunches; Mom shut away; little sister Akane is frail). Nanoha, the girl next door since birth, walks in without knocking, brings food, does deadpan bits with him privately.
- Natsuki transferred in this morning: confident self-intro; when her eyes reached Kōhei, her grin stuttered for a second. Passing his desk, she guessed he does kendo from the callus on his left palm ("Takes one to know one"). He couldn't concentrate all period.

**Beats that must survive (story plants used later):**
1. Natsuki is a social natural: by lunch she's met half the class, "Sendai, most recently," invited to clubs, karaoke.
2. She comes to Kōhei's desk and brings up that he turned down three confessions last year, all with "no time for a girlfriend"; he confirms he doesn't have time.
3. She sizes him up; competitive kendo banter (his rank; "we'll find out on the floor"); she likes that he's cocky.
4. Club starts Wednesday; she repeats "Wednesday" and the narration says she repeats it "like she's writing it on the inside of her wrist" (a deliberate wrist echo of the prologue; keep that image).
5. Nanoha approaches, brave-formal, offers to show Natsuki around after school (mentions she was the class representative's assistant last year and still has the clipboard; the stuck shoe lockers and the coin-eating vending machine).
6. Natsuki is startled to be the one asked, and touched; accepts warmly.
7. Names: Natsuki asks to be called Natsuki; Nanoha manages only "Natsuki-san" and says she needs time; Natsuki will call her "Nanoha" anyway. (Japanese honorific dynamics matter.)
8. Kōhei, under the two of them laughing in the window light, feels like furniture. Ends on him eating his rice ("It's very good rice. I made it myself.").
