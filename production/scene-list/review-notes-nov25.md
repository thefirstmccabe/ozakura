## Reviewed so far: Ch1–6 (Nov 25)

**Approved as proposed:** 1.1 (Akane sends them ahead; Emi goes to her entrance ceremony), 1.3 (Takatsuki's taiyaki entrance, no Ryōsuke in Ch1, Rika cut to about half), 5.1 (the future-plans form as the frame for Kōhei's goal), 5.3 (Akane as the kendo club's manager). 3.1: he doesn't mind where the dizzy spell happens, so Story decides.

**Decided by the author:**
- **The opening.** The cold open becomes a real scene, "Chapter 0": Takatsuki and Akane walking and talking first, then the road goes wrong. Then **opening credits with a song** (stills and short clips, cut fast), ending on new title art, then Ch1. He wants the opening good enough to hook people.
  - **Flow he agreed to:** first visit, a nearly bare start screen with one tap and no logo or art; Chapter 0, cutting to black on the flashlights; the opening credits, ending on the title; hold on the title with one button to begin Ch1. Every later visit opens on that title screen as the menu, with Continue and a way to replay the opening.
  - **New title art:** all five of the core cast, with a little more dread.
  - **Cautions agreed:** nothing in the credits may show who the dream woman is, and both girls get equal weight. Canon §2 says no cutscenes and needs a line allowing this. The build belongs to a design pass, the Audio thread and Production, after the scene list is approved.
  - Chapter 0 itself: ideally animated; most likely a color manga sequence with sound, with animation tried on one or two moments [P].
- **4.2: Mio asks.** Natsuki would already know they aren't dating (she wouldn't stay at his job until close otherwise), so the question can't be hers. Mio, tagging along to the study group: "Kō-nii, when are you marrying Onee-chan?" His "We're not" still comes too fast. What Natsuki learns is how fast he says it and what it does to Nanoha, which only she sees. 4.3's text to Chie changes to match (no "update: not dating").
- **Kōhei half-knows how Nanoha feels.** "With how his life is right now he's really scared to let anything between them change because she's the stabilizing presence… so he basically doesn't let himself realize even though he kinda does." For canon §6 and §8; it's why the denial is so fast.
- **3.4: the snow.** A split-second CG of the tree in snow, not a smell. He definitely sees it; it isn't clear whether the girls do; nobody says anything.
- **3.4: the light moves to Ch4.** The light and "…You came back" leave the tree scene and go to Nanoha's window on the night of "We're not," leading straight into the memory. (This changes the built Ch3.)
- **3.2: the unspoken Saturday goes.** "Never worked for me; it's crazy subtle." Nanoha just tells him Natsuki is coming. (This changes the built Ch3.)
- **Akane and Takatsuki.** Her crush predates the story, and she has known him for years, so he and Kōhei go back to middle school [P]. She's herself with him most of the time (cheerful, teasing, stubborn) and short-circuits when he looks straight at her or praises her. Not bashful all the time. 4.1 is no longer where her interest starts.
- **Less of them in Part 1.** Five beats in five chapters was too many. Keep 4.1, 5.3 and 6.2 (the stopwatch). **5.7** loses its Akane–Takatsuki bit and **8.2** (the inventory) goes [Story's pick]. In their place: **Kōhei and Takatsuki being friends** (5.7: the two of them going at each other in the arcade; 8.2: a scene of the two of them [P]). 5.7 can also carry some mild Nanoha–Natsuki friction.
- **5.2:** Nanoha already knows about the three confessions, so Rika's point is that Natsuki is the bigger problem.
- **On screen:** a CG of Natsuki scoring in 3.3; a Street Brawlers CG in 3.6; 4.4's memory as manga; 5.5's backyard training maybe as manga; 2.6 stays a still.
- **5.4** stays: he asked whether it's separate from the meet in Ch12, and it is (a small private warning before the public one).

## Reviewed: Ch15–21 (Nov 25, evening)

Everything in Ch15–21 not listed here he marked keep. He asked for critique of his own suggestions, so several of these were settled after pushback.

**Decided by the author:**
- **17.4: the stone is for both of them.** (Settles canon's Open item on whose memorial it is.) How, as a proposal [P]: she outlived him, raised it for him, and her name was added when she died old. Nothing on it a reader could take as proof. At Obon Natsuki doesn't yet know who she was, so she is weeding her own grave without knowing it.
- **18.1** (the tournament) becomes manga. With V2 gone it pairs with the street fight: the loss by the rules, then the win without them.
- **18.2:** the girls know how he's doing, he knows they know, and their pity makes him angrier. A plain internal monologue of anger over his life. Story's guard: the anger points outward (work, money, his mother, no practice); he doesn't know he's dangerous until 18.4.
- **18.3:** the proposed escalation is approved (words, then a hand on her arm). "There's a sense in which he is a little bit too eager to fight… glad he has an excuse." Shown, not narrated: he steps in faster than he needs to and lets the man swing first [P].
