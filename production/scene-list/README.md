## 2. What to make
A new living doc, **`story/scene-list.md`** (mirrored to the Project as `ozakura/scene-list.md`): every scene of the whole story. Add it to the doc lists in `CLAUDE.md` and the production log, and tell the author to add it to the project instructions.

**Work in passes, and don't write the full version first:**

- **Pass 2, Part by Part, in full** (§3), after Pass 1 is settled. Each Part goes through outline readers (§5) before it goes to him.

## 3. A scene entry (Pass 2)
Keep every entry the same shape and short enough to scan on an iPad.

- **ID and title.** `chapter.scene` (12.3). **IDs never renumber:** a scene added later gets a letter (12.3a), a cut scene leaves a gap. His feedback is keyed to these.
- **When, where, who is on screen.**
- **What happens:** two or three plain lines.
- **What changes:** one line (a relationship, what someone knows, the plot). A scene with nothing to put here doesn't belong.
- **Whose scene:** Nanoha's, Natsuki's or neither; and any uncanny beat and whose it is.
- **Threads:** triangle, dread, family, Akane and Takatsuki, kendo, a comic situation.
- **On screen as:** sprites (the default), CG (how many), manga insert, gag insert, living still, a short animation attempt, or an imagined scene. He asked for this: "Is it going to outline suggested areas for CGs, manga, or at least attempts at animation?" Use CGs far more freely than a traditional VN would; cost isn't the limit.
- **Source:** Decided (author), canon scene bank number, or **[P]** for your invention.
- **Needs his call:** yes or no, and the question in one line.

Above the scenes, each chapter gets one sentence saying what it changes, and each Part gets one paragraph.

## 4. Tables (end of Pass 1, and again per Part)
- **Presence grid:** the core five and the side cast against chapters. This is the evidence for whether anyone vanishes; it's why Ryōsuke and Rika were stepped back.
- **The triangle by chapter:** whose chapter, and Natsuki's present-day closeness.
- **Dread and uncanny beats** by chapter and owner.
- **Presentation counts:** CGs, inserts and animation attempts per chapter.
- **Everything that needs his call,** in one list, in story order.

## 5. Rules for the content
- **Length:** no longer than the current plan. The pilot chapters run about 3,000 words and 7–8 scenes; the spine is 44 chapters. He doesn't want a longer story, and you may propose a shorter one.
- **Focus on the five.** Ryōsuke (class president), Rika (the gossipy, romance-minded friend), Kirishima, Yuzu and the rest are side schoolmates and adults who appear when a scene needs them, and aren't introduced as major.
- **More Akane** (author). She's a first-year at their school, Nanoha's romantic confidante, and can eat lunch with them. She needs something she wants for herself.
- **Humor comes from situations and reactions.** Mark the comic situations. A bit that earns it can be a gag insert in a different art style.
- **A hint is only for a real mystery or major foreshadowing.** Ordinary facts are shown plainly.
- **Reader targets** (canon §5 as updated Nov 25): the two invariants are hard; balance is budgeted here, per arc; by default one uncanny owner per chapter, with exceptions named.
- **Never settle an item canon marks Open.** Propose, mark it [P], and put it on the needs-his-call list. Don't stop at each one; he's the bottleneck, so draft through and collect them.
- **Outline readers:** before a Part goes to him, fresh agents who haven't seen it written read it as a treatment and report where it sags, who has been absent too long, where the triangle stalls, and anything confusing. He approved fresh-agent checks as standing practice.

## 6. How he gives feedback (design this with care)
His words: "it would be great if it's organized in a way where I can tap to speak in any particular section about edits, or at least write edits I'd want. So think carefully about the output and how it would be easiest for me to give my feedback."

What's known about him: he works on an iPad and a phone; he dictates; he reads for the shape and won't mark up line by line; pages built as artifacts didn't play for him in October, while plain web pages on the project's site did (style guide §8).

**Proposal [P], yours to improve:**
- **A review page** generated from `scene-list.md`: one card per scene, collapsible by chapter and Part. Each card has a note field, which takes the iPad keyboard's dictation key, and three one-tap marks: keep, change, cut. Chapters and Parts get a note field too.
- Notes save in the browser as he goes. **One button at the end copies every note with its scene ID,** ready to paste into this thread.
- **Prove the loop on one chapter first.** Before Pass 1, give him one existing chapter (Ch3) in this format and confirm that tapping, dictating and exporting work on his iPad.
- **Ask him where the page may live.** The repo and its site are public, so a page there publishes the whole plot. Canon is already public by his Oct 10 decision, but on Nov 24 he said he may want a wider release someday.
- He can always answer in chat by ID instead ("12.3: cut").

The in-game flag button planned for chapters (`handoff-planning-nov22.md` §6) is the same idea; build this so that work can reuse it.
