## 2. Line-by-line verdicts (flagged lines only; everything else is KEEP)

### Prologue
1. **31** "She says it like she's been waiting a very long time." **FLAT.** It states the hook, which 36–37 then state again, and it adds a "very".
2. **33** "…like the knot matters more than anything else in the world." **FLAT.** A generic simile. "Slow and careful" already shows the care.
3. **36–37** "That someone was waiting for me. / That I kept them waiting far too long." **CUT.** Line 35 says it's a feeling "I can't put a name to", and then he names it right away. The outline asks for "only the feeling".

The sheet's own Akane line ("you're hovering") does the same job.

**Humor mechanisms (§3b).**
- **Kōhei:** a good straight man in plain moments (80, 114, 119, 121, 258, 265, 275, 360). He drifts into topping and polished replies exactly where his sheet says he goes shorter: under Natsuki's flirting (269, 273) and Nanoha's tenderness (137, 154), plus 70.
- **Nanoha:** her private mode is right (the theory, keeping score, the catalog). Her public mode with Natsuki has one dry joke too many (283 and 287).
- **Natsuki:** her mechanism appears once, in the callus line (242), and offscreen in the stairs (304, 349–350). At lunch she runs on verdicts and self-labels and borrows Nanoha's "very X" deadpan (270, 328).
- **Akane:** right in 63, 67, 73 and 98. She leaks into Nanoha's mock-formal register (65, 71).
- **Rika:** right throughout.
- **Ryōsuke:** the right form (pedantry) on a banned topic, with two non-pedant zingers (179, 191).

**Narration repeating or vouching.** "Says it / said it" glosses appear 8 times: 30, 31, 74, 147, 271, 277, 317, 353. The lint counts 3 because it only matches the exact phrase. Explanatory followers that restate the previous line: 201, 285, 320, 329, 337. The prologue's triple "She says it like…" works as a deliberate figure. Keep it as the chapter's one use, and keep the outline's 336 as the one "the way you".

**Tics from §3b's list.**
- "Very" in spoken lines appears 8 times across Akane, Nanoha (4) and Natsuki (2). The limit is about 3. Narration adds 2 more (297, 304).
- Face-reading: Akane at 67–69 and 96, and Nanoha at 93–95 and 356–361.
- "Says it like": 3 by exact match, 8 with variants.
- "The way you": 336 and 353.
- American-internet bits: the hot dog and "sub roll" (174–183), "That's what they want you to think" (124), the jet-lag routine (326–328).
- Not on the list but repeated: "Something about" at 271 and 295; "longer than the X needs/requires" at 134 and 371; "They both laugh(ed)" at 295 and 329.

**Address and register (§3c).**
- 366: "Chibi" is missing (verdict 51).
- 282: Natsuki says きみは to Nanoha. *Kimi* is her signature for Kōhei only; she uses names for everyone else. The English is fine.
- 284 and 287: Nanoha uses plain forms with someone she has just met (案内しようか, 大したツアーじゃないよ). §3c gives her polite desu/masu with strangers. Her plain よ at 294, after Natsuki insists, is fine.
- 210: Ōno's English "I" (verdict 22).

**Outline ending (1.7).** "Nanoha: 'You smiled.' The chapter ends there." The script runs eight more lines of bit, a button, the good night and the lit window. You can read the outline as ending the chapter at that scene rather than on the exact line, so the good night and window can stay. But the catalog's "number one / Secret" button and the 371 tag dilute the last sincere beat.

## 4. The five highest-value fixes (ranked)

1. **Take Nanoha's deadpan back from everyone else.** No "I'm very X", mock-formal tags or deadpan tails outside Nanoha (65, 71, 191, 262, 270, 328), and bring the chapter's spoken "very" down to three or fewer.
2. **Rebuild Natsuki's lunch turns around questions and physical noticing, with the callus line as the model.** Remove every verdict and self-label (268, 270, 272, 274, 289).
3. **When he's flirted with or fussed over, Kōhei goes shorter and plays straight.** Strip his toppers and polished replies (70, 137, 154, 269, 273).
4. **Swap Ryōsuke's hot-dog/sub-roll entrance for the taiyaki head-or-tail argument the sheet names.** He should argue it with total seriousness and no zingers (179).
5. **Cut the narration that explains the line before it** (36–37, 147, 201, 271, 337, 353). Keep the prologue's "says it like" triplet and the outline's window line as the chapter's single uses.

**Required regardless of ranking:**
- "Night, Chibi" (おやすみ、チビ) at 366.
- Natsuki uses Nanoha's name, not きみ, at 282.
- Nanoha's polite form at 284 and 287.
- Akane's face-reading becomes "hovering".
