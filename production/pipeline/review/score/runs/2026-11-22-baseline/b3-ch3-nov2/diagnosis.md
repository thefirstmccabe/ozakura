**Totals:** 28 lines flagged (21 FLAT, 7 CUT), plus 3 scene-level problems not tied to a single line (S5, S6, S7 below). **31 problems.**

**The brief's hard rules hold:** Kōhei never says "Nanoha" aloud; no hair color; no "You're late"; Nanoha doesn't catalogue his faces; "very" appears once, and that's the required "very convenient back"; every face used is on the list; deadpan and smirk faces only play punchlines; Japanese address (Natsuki's *kimi*, お前, Mio's third person, Takatsuki avoiding "you") matches §3c. ## 3. Scene-level problems

**S1. Buttons.** Five of seven scenes end on a crafted line: "Then I'm taking the long way." (dojo), 0367 (steps), "I'm almost sure it's real." (tree; that one earns it), "Who's Mio? Is she good?" (tea), 0308 (lunch). The two scenes that end quietly, "…Window." and "I leave my window open a while longer.", are the chapter's best endings. Inside scenes, the home scene runs three topper exchanges in a row (0073 → 0084/0085 → 0087/0088), and the window scene stacks one Nanoha punchline after another (0108, 0112, 0116, 0118, 0126). Exchanges that miss or go unanswered are rare. Takatsuki bowing out (0334) and Genji's "Looks like rain tomorrow." are the models.

**S2. Feelings stated.** Dialogue mostly behaves. "You're not the only person who loves her, Kōhei." is properly adjacent, nobody names their own feeling, and Natsuki's project line is short and sincere as the sheet asks. **The narration is where feelings get stated:** 0080 (it landed), 0120 (only I get this version of her), 0367 (she made me stop being tired), 0373 (she knew and didn't tell me), 0308 (I don't believe it). Each line explains a beat the scene has already shown.

**S3. Humor mechanisms (§3b).**
- **Kōhei:** on sheet. He's the literal straight man ("I'm kneeling.", "…Four and a half.", "It's trash bags.", "Yes, ma'am."), goes practical ("I take the trash bags."), and goes short under sincerity ("Delete that.").
- **Nanoha (private):** on sheet. The Usagi-san bit is committed deadpan; 0064 is mock-formal; 0432 is a practical-detail dodge. The one weak point is 0082's slang.
- **Nanoha (public): the main voice failure.** The chapter never gives her a single hesitation, no "Um—" and no "If you'd like…". At the shrine and the invitation she is brisk, confident and bossy (0273, 0278), and she uses her private mock-formal register on Natsuki (0278). "Rude" (0196) and "It's a sickness" (0274) are fine as her one understatement per scene; 0278 is extra.
- **Natsuki:** mostly on sheet. Her physical reads (heel, sleep, left hand, the kendo-glove smell), short-sincere-then-own-history (Niigata), and casual cover for a big ask ("I'm going that way anyway.") are all hers. She thins out into generic quips at 0333, 0364, 0371 and 0390.
- **Akane:** sibling teasing is good ("hovering", "'a bad Wednesday'"). 0073 drifts into Nanoha's deadpan.
- **Mio, Genji, Takatsuki, Kirishima, Emi:** on sheet. The only miss is Mio's English 0292.

**S4. Narration repeats or vouches:** 0074 (repeats 0067), 0311 (repeats 0310 and Akane), 0085 (tags the joke), 0088 ("Conversation over."), 0368 (repeats 0181), 0373 (connects dots), 0400 (tone tag), 0080 (vouches).

**S5. Takatsuki's tip vs the one-sided read** (problem not tied to a line). The beat is that he can't read her. Then 0332 hands Kōhei her tell ("Mizuno still drops her left shoulder before {men|head strikes}. You did not see it once."), and 0329's "I watch her shoulders" becomes a setup for the correction. The line is funny and on-voice, but it converts the asymmetry into "he can't read her, but a third party can and just told him." Check it against the outline's Ch3 targets. If the one-sidedness has to carry into later sparring, soften the tip (e.g., Takatsuki says only that a tell exists).

**S6. Tic: "perfectly" ×3** (problem not tied to a line): 0119 "perfectly straight," 0267 "perfectly good smile," 0306 "Perfectly ordinary." The first two use up the closer's word. Change the earlier two. Also in the closer, "Above us" (0306) comes after Natsuki has left, so "us" is unclear.

**S7. Takatsuki's English "you"** (minor, not tied to a line). §3c says he avoids "you," and the JP does (ふじさわは). The English 0332 says "you" twice ("You did not see it once. I am telling you…"). Mirror the JP if it reads naturally.

**Other §3b tics:**
- "very": one use (required). Passes.
- "She says it…": one use (0347, "She says it fast and quiet…"), which is the chapter's allowance. 0398 ("Nanoha says it right behind him") is a near-second; watch it.
- "the way…": 0430 and 0306 sit three lines apart. Mild; watch it.
- Two girls reading his face: Natsuki only. Passes.
- Americanisms: flagged at 0082 and 0276. 0289 ("I main"), 0292 and 0407 ("Yes, ma'am") are tolerable on their own, but together they pull away from the translated feel.
- Near-miss on "nobody explains their own culture": 0064 ("'Welcome home' is customarily answered with 'I'm home.'") survives because the joke is her pedantry. Make sure Production doesn't also gloss it.

## 4. Five highest-value fixes (ranked)

1. **Cut 0373 ("Thursday. After school. Before the window.").** Let Natsuki's "on Thursday" and "I take the trash bags." carry the double silence unremarked, as the brief requires.
2. **Rebuild Nanoha's invitation (0273, 0278) in her public register.** Give her a hesitation so her bravery reads as politeness. Keep "It's a sickness" as her one dry line, and answer "…Really? I can come?" with something gentle or with an action instead of the mock-official "It's decided."
3. **Strip the home scene's buttons:** cut 0084 and 0085 and the tag "Conversation over." in 0088, so "…Next time, call me anyway." interrupts an unfinished joke instead of following a punchline.
4. **Cut the untaken-photo exchange (0392–0393)** unless the outline calls for it. The narration already landed it, and her cover story makes a quiet image into a flagged mystery and a new signal.
5. **End scenes on images, not verdicts:** drop 0367 and 0308, and trim 0120 to its image, so the steps and "Perfectly ordinary." carry their own weight. Fix the "perfectly" ×3 (S6) in the same pass.
