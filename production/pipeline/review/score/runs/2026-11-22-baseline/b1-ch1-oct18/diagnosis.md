# Dialogue diagnosis: Ch1, Oct 18 revision (§9 step 3c, part 1)

**Read:** style guide §3b–§3c; brief `briefs/b1-ch1-oct18.md`; `production/pilot-v2/script/ch01.ozs` (whole chapter); lint `lint/b1-ch1-oct18.txt`.
**Rule applied:** lines the brief marks decided or must-survive, and all of 1.8, are KEEP. Where I flag a line next to a protected beat, the beat stays and only the flagged wording changes.

**Bottom line.** This draft fixes most of what the Oct 17 cold reads complained about. Akane no longer reads as a younger Nanoha, Natsuki's noticing works (the callus, her posture question, the stairs, the bathroom window), and Nanoha's bits are intact. Five problems are left:
1. Natsuki's one comic exchange with Kōhei is a quip duel that breaks both their speech sheets.
2. The interlude's narration explains what its best lines already show.
3. The class-list beat has a logic hole.
4. The last line before the dream points the dream at Natsuki.
5. About a third of the exchanges still end on a topper, and Ryōsuke is sliding back toward the dry-quip voice.

**Count:** 21 line-level flags (17 FLAT, 4 CUT) and 8 scene-level problems. Some scene-level items group line flags together.

## 2. Line-by-line verdicts

### 1.1 Morning (c1_0001–c1_0051)
- **c1_0016** akane "And I slept! Super well! I didn't wake up once!" **FLAT.** Off-voice: Rika's exclamation stack. Keep the protesting-too-much and change the delivery.
- **c1_0025** > "She says it to the rice cooker, not to me. I pretend I didn't hear that." **FLAT.** It explains itself. The rice cooker shows it; "I pretend I didn't hear that" tells it. It also uses the "She says it…" frame, which the dream owns.
- **c1_0044** akane "He totally did." **FLAT.** Generic and off-voice.
- **c1_0051** > "That's an answer. Sort of." **FLAT.** Too tidy: a wry button on the quietest beat in the chapter, when the creak is already the answer.
- Worth protecting: c1_0024 "…You always are." is the best Akane line in this build, the quiet sincere line under the teasing that her sheet asks for.

### 1.2 River walk (c1_0052–c1_0098)

## 3. Scene-level problems

1. **Most exchanges still end on a neat button.** The lines that don't are the chapter's best moments: "…You always are.", "Mm.", the 水, "…Really?", "Anyway. Thanks." The worst stretch is 1.3: after Rika's tackle, nearly every line is a punchline ("Hmm-hmm-hmmm!", "It's thermodynamics.", "Oh no.", "I'll need a diagram.", "covers most of the planet," "narrowed it down"), and only Kōhei's lines aren't. The end of 1.2 stacks them too: "very convenient back," then "trash bags while you supervise," then "supervise beautifully." Scenes 1.1 and 1.5 both close on a narration tag (c1_0051, c1_0202).
2. **Feelings stated, and narration vouching for the dialogue.** The dialogue is mostly clean. Kōhei never names a feeling, Natsuki's loneliness comes out sideways (the drawer), and Nanoha's love hides inside her bits. The leaks are in the narration: c1_0025, c1_0131, c1_0215, c1_0227 and c1_0234 each tell the reader what the line before just showed. The interlude is worst: three of its last ten narration lines explain. Nanoha's "I like her" (c1_0242) also repeats c1_0232. c1_0187 ("about to do something brave") is borderline; keep it, because it's Kōhei knowing her tells, not a gloss.
3. **Humor mechanisms, character by character:**
   - **Nanoha:** right (committed bits, theories, keeping score). In public mode she goes over her one dry line (c1_0193).
   - **Natsuki:** right when she's noticing things (callus, posture, stairs, "normal face"); off-sheet in the lunch duel (c1_0182).
   - **Akane:** right (sibling teasing, cheerful defiance, the sincere line under it). The "younger Nanoha" problem is fixed; what's left drifts toward Rika (c1_0016) or a generic teen (c1_0044).
   - **Rika:** right, except c1_0126, which is a wit's comeback.
   - **Ryōsuke:** the head-first argument is his. The cappers (c1_0111, c1_0125) are Nanoha's rhythm coming back, which is exactly what the Oct 17 reads complained about.
4. **Kōhei has one move.** The literal retort: "You're already in." "I'm standing in my own kitchen." "Meetings." "The pool is green." "It's seven-fifty in the morning." "That's what the attendance sheet says." "I don't have a face." "I'm moving it closer to the right day." Each one works. Together they make him a setup machine for five different partners, and Natsuki gets the same opener and response as everyone else (c1_0166). The one time he changes for her, he gets cooler (c1_0181) when his sheet says shorter. His best lines with her are the broken ones ("…How did you—", "I—", "Who told you that?"). He needs more of those and fewer retorts.
5. **Kōhei barely gets to see Natsuki's charm, and the brief's main goal is at risk.** Nanoha gets six built bits that land on Kōhei directly (the theory, the minutes, the score, "short-person direction," the convenient back, the catalogue), plus two tender beats (the collar, the schedule). Natsuki's direct material with him is:
   - the callus (excellent);
   - the questions about the confessions (required, though it opens on gossip, which reads a little nosy);
   - the rank-and-posture duel (off-sheet).

   Her most likable moments (the bathroom window "Oh," the haunted-building "Perfect.", the drawer, the smaller smile) all happen in the interlude, through Nanoha's eyes. That's fine for an interlude, but lunch is where Natsuki has to win the reader on her own, and its comic peak is the weakest writing in her part.
6. **The dream points at one girl.** The setting cues are Nanoha's (the river, petals, a branch: the river walk and the petal on her braid). The body cues are Natsuki's (a kendo cut, a hand, the inside of the wrist, after the decided 水). That balance is probably deliberate. c1_0264 breaks it by putting a Natsuki-only image last before the dream begins.
7. **Logic hole in the class lists.** In c1_0102 Rika tells Nanoha her class in front of Kōhei, which undercuts c1_0129 and the inference in c1_0131. This is one of the beats that must survive ("Nanoha finding his name first and there being no Fukuda in 2-B"), and right now it doesn't hold up.
8. **Tics (§3b list):**
   - *"She says it like…":* appears only in the dream (deliberate; the lint flag is a false positive). But the same delivery gloss shows up twice outside the dream in other wording, c1_0025 "She says it to the rice cooker" and c1_0215 "Nanoha said it as lightly as she knew how." That uses up the device before the dream gets it.
   - *"the way you…":* once (c1_0233, protected), which is within the limit. But "the way she/he…" clusters around it: c1_0017, c1_0109, c1_0187. Watch for it.
   - *"very":* twice, both protected (c1_0095, p_0009). The "I'm very X" joke survives in another form as "I do supervise beautifully." (c1_0097).
   - *Two girls reading his face:* passes. Nanoha reads his face (c1_0043, c1_0246–c1_0251); Natsuki reads his hands and posture. c1_0224 ("He's got kind of a normal one") is about his face but doesn't read it. Don't add any more.
   - *American internet bits:* nothing on the scale of the hot-dog debate. Smaller American imports: "He totally did." (c1_0044), "Big talk for someone who…" (c1_0182), and the "it's science" shape of "That isn't an opinion. It's thermodynamics." (c1_0111).
   - *Repeated construction:* "longer than the collar strictly needs" (c1_0076) and "longer than the garbage requires" (c1_0259).

## 4. The five highest-value fixes (ranked)

1. **Rebuild the lunch rank-and-posture beat (c1_0180–c1_0182) on Natsuki's own mechanism,** one more question or noticed detail instead of a verdict, and have Kōhei get shorter instead of cooler. It's her only comic set piece with him and the brief's top priority.
2. **Move the callus beat (c1_0264) away from the start of the dream** so that 1.7 ends on her light across the gap and nothing Natsuki-only leads into the hands binding his wrist.
3. **Strip the interlude's explaining narration:** cut c1_0234, cut the delivery gloss in c1_0215, and cut "Natsuki laughed first. The moment closed up" from c1_0227. Let the window image and "It was fine." carry it.
4. **Take Nanoha's class out of Rika's tackle (c1_0102)** so that "You found mine before yours?" and the Fukuda reveal work. Then drop the narration's spelled-out inference at the end of c1_0131.
5. **Let three exchanges miss instead of landing a joke:** cut c1_0097 ("I do supervise beautifully."), c1_0193 (the vending-machine button that answers Natsuki's "…Really?") and c1_0126 ("narrowed it down"). Turn Ryōsuke's c1_0125 back into pedantry he believes.
