## Problems, most important first

### 1. Natsuki critiques how he strikes men before she has seen him strike anyone (who knows what; breaks the Ch2 "Not yet")
- **Where:** ch02.ozs:169 [c2_0125]. "Left heel, a little low. The tip wanders. **He hits men from the wrong distance and gets away with it**, which is the worst possible thing for a first-year to see."
- **Why:** at this point in the session she has only seen warm-ups and suburi (110 "Warm-ups and suburi. Bogu after the break."; 122 "At the water break after warm-ups"). Drills and the match come after the break (183–184). You can't judge striking distance without an opponent, and "gets away with it" means she has seen it work. Her own interlude explains her reading of him with "she'd watched him all through the drills" (371), and those drills haven't happened yet. A careful reader can only explain the line by past-life knowledge (canon §5: "She watched him train for years"). That breaks the outline's Ch2 "Not yet: any sign of fragments of her own; anything supernatural on screen" (scene-outline:169).
- **Fix (pick one):** (a) Cut the sentence and keep the two faults you can see in a stance (heel, tip). (b) Give it to Takatsuki, who has trained with him for a year: *takatsuki: "He also strikes men from the wrong distance. It works. It should not."* That fits his speech sheet and sets up 4.5. (c) Make it an obvious guess: "And I bet he hits men from too far out and gets away with it." Pilot-notes §3 already records the fault list. Only who says it, and when, needs to change.

### 2. The debana setup stacks the uncanny higher than the outline asks (reader target; watch the dream-woman number)

The acceptable wrong guess (she fought beside him) still holds, so this is a calibration risk, not a hard break.
- **Fix:** cut 157 and Takatsuki's lesson line at 159. Keep debana as her favorite (logged as [P], see problem 13) and keep the debrief at 232–233, which is the outline beat. The match then reads as skill plus something, and the cold reads decide whether more is needed.
