# Ch3 "Windows": dialogue diagnosis (§9 step 3c, part 1)

Build: b3-ch3-nov2. Draft: `production/pilot-v2/script/ch03.ozs` (229 spoken and narration lines). Lint: 0 flags.

**Brief compliance (quick check):** Kōhei never says "Nanoha" aloud. Hair color never comes up, there's no "You're late", neither of them mentions Saturday at the window, and "He knows what he did" isn't said. "Very" appears once (the back), "Chibi" is the only height beat, and every face used is on the list. So the problems are in rhythm, a few voices that have drifted, and some additions that aren't in the brief.

**Bottom line:** The core holds. That includes the 3.1 exchange through "It lands somewhere I wasn't guarding.", the rabbit bit, and on the steps the sincere line, "It's trash bags." and the Niigata laugh line. The snow fragment, the rest phrase with the weather dodge, and the lunch all hold too. There are four main faults:
1. An inserted clinic-and-Dad passage turns the window scene into a briefing.
2. Nearly every exchange ends on a neat button, and four of the seven scenes end on the same kind of one-line Kōhei narration.
3. Natsuki reads Kōhei in Nanoha's cadence.
4. An added tea exchange turns the approved silent photo beat into a spoken cover story.

## 3. Scene-level problems

**3.1 Does every exchange end on a neat button? Nearly.**
- The buttons, in order: 0085, then 0087 with 0088 (the deal plus "Conversation over."); then 0126, 0436, 0333, 0340, 0367, 0391, 0393, 0291 with 0292, and 0308.
- Four of the seven scenes close on a one-line Kōhei narration of the same shape: 0323 "I leave my window open a while longer.", 0367, 0267 "…I'm almost sure it's real.", and 0308. Each one is fine on its own; four is a tic. Keep 0323 and 0267.
- The exchanges that end open are the chapter's best: 0131 (his silence), 0281 "Who's Mio? Is she good?" and 0421 "Have some {karaage|fried chicken}, Onii-chan." Use them as the model.

**3.2 Are feelings stated instead of shown? Around the approved lines, yes.**
- The approved sincere lines work because they're aimed at something next to the real feeling: 0130's "loves her" is about Akane, and 0346 is about a "project".
- The stating happens around them:
  - The clinic-and-Dad passage (0432–0437) explains Kōhei's fear after 3.1 has shown it.
  - 0120 explains private Nanoha after the bit has shown her.
  - 0367 tells us Natsuki lifted his tiredness.
  - 0373 tells us to connect Thursday to the window.
  - 0308 tells us he doesn't believe "ordinary."
- 0080 ("It lands somewhere I wasn't guarding.") is the right way to do it: specific, from kendo, no label. The others should meet that bar or go.

**3.3 Does narration repeat or vouch for the dialogue? Yes, in four places.**
- 0311 repeats 0310.
- 0085 and 0088 put buttons on the dialogue they follow.
- 0400 vouches for a tone the line already carries.
- 0347 ("She says it fast and quiet, to the step in front of her.") is the counterexample to copy: it gives the delivery, not an interpretation.

**3.4 Does each character use their own humor mechanism (§3b)?**
- **Kōhei: yes.** Literal answers (0072, 0349, 0356), understatement (0378), mock seriousness (0420), playing along (0407, 0115). He goes shorter under sincerity (0363). Only one line borrows Natsuki's move, an opening jab (0330), which is fine once.
- **Nanoha, private: yes** (0064, 0084, 0105–0118, 0315). The strongest voice in the chapter.
- **Nanoha, public: over budget.** Her sheet allows one dry understatement at most, with small hesitations. With Natsuki present across the tree and tea scenes she gets 0196 ("Rude"), 0404, 0274, 0278 and 0280, and no hesitation anywhere, not even in the invitation (0271, 0273).
- **Natsuki: half right.**
  - Right: the laugh line from her own history (0350) and the trash talk after she wins (0425) are exactly her sheet.
  - Wrong: her noticing has the right content but the wrong form. All three reads (0331, 0355, 0357) are statements, which is Nanoha's mode, and 0357's counting is Nanoha's catalogue.
  - Her one flirt (0340) is polished, and her one reaction to Takatsuki (0333) is generic.
- **Akane: drifts.** 0073 is a Nanoha topper. Her 3.1 lines are flat statements (0075), and her exaggeration lives in Nanoha's report of her (0066, "should be arrested") instead of in her own mouth. Being sleepy covers some of that, but not 0073. Akane at lunch (0417, 0419, 0421) is right.
- **The rest: right.** Genji (0377, 0379, 0401) except 0391; Mio, Emi, Kirishima and Takatsuki.

**3.5 Tics and §3b rules**
- **"Very":** once (0379, required). Clean.
- **"She says it like…" / "the way you…":** none. Clean.
- **Two girls reading his face:** only Natsuki does. Clean in letter, but 0357 hands Natsuki Nanoha's catalogue, which is exactly what the rule is there to prevent.
- **American-internet feel:** 0082 "It wasn't a thing." 0276's "picked first in gym" is mild (Japanese PE rarely picks teams that way). 0289's "I main Kagero" is acceptable gamer English.
- **"Nobody explains their own culture":** 0064 ("'Welcome home' is customarily answered with 'I'm home.'") works as a bit, because it tells him everything's fine before he asks. In English it also works as a gloss of okaeri and tadaima. Low priority; see what the cold readers say.
- **Word echoes:**
  - "Perfectly" three times (0119 "perfectly straight", 0267 "perfectly good smile", 0306 "Perfectly ordinary."). The first two water down the closing line.
  - "Ten" four times in 3.1 (0058, 0059, 0074, 0075).
  - "Look" five times (0365–0366), "come up" twice (0321), and "drama"/"dramatic" (0060).

**3.6 Lines that put the brief's beats at risk** (for the Story or Production thread to decide; not rewrites)
- **0332:** Takatsuki hands Kōhei her tell "so that it is fair." It's in voice and funny, but the outline wants the reading to be one-sided, and the whole point of this line is to even it up. Either cut it, or make sure nothing later lets Kōhei use the tell.
- **0392–0393:** risks a new "met him before" signal (see the tea table).
- **0106 ("with his back to me"):** a time-out with his back turned is one step from Ch4's face-the-wall. It complies in letter. Make sure `cg_window_sill` doesn't read as the rabbit facing a wall, or Ch4's image is used up early.
- **0432–0437:** not among the approved 3.2 beats. If they serve a reader-knowledge target I can't see from here, keep the information but drop the briefing form. Dad's cause of death (0437) needs a canon check before it ships.
- **0373:** the narration remarks on a silence the brief says nobody remarks on.

## 4. The five highest-value fixes (ranked)

1. **Cut 0125–0126 and 0432–0437** so "I know. That's why." runs straight into the wind and the cat (0127). If the medical facts must be in Ch3, give Nanoha one concrete detail. Move the cancer fact out of this scene after a canon check.
2. **Break the button rhythm.**
   - Let three exchanges end unanswered: cut "Only if you stop hovering." and "Conversation over." (0087, 0088) so the blanket answers him; cut 0340 so "The station's the other way." gets no reply; cut 0333 so Takatsuki's bow ends it.
   - Drop two of the four closing narration buttons (0367 and 0308).
3. **Give Natsuki's reads back to her.** Turn at least two of 0331, 0355 and 0357 into questions or pokes, and drop the counting ("Every ten steps or so", "Third period").
4. **Cut the tea exchange about the untaken shot (0392–0393) and Genji's "This one'll do." (0391).** The photo beat stays silent, and the tea ends on her cup held out for a third.
5. **Put public Nanoha back on her sheet.** Cut 0280. Keep "Rude" and "It's a sickness" as her dry lines. Give the invitation (0271, 0273) one small hesitation so it reads as the brave act it is.
