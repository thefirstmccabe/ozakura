## 3. Scene-level problems

**Neat buttons.** Almost every comic exchange ends on a capper:
- "It's a sickness"
- "Traitor." / "Nanoha-chan brought kinpira!"
- "That's what it says in the minutes" → "two points" → "You're losing"
- "I do supervise beautifully"
- "Hmm-hmm-hmmm!"
- "Please don't."
- "narrowed it down"
- "Big talk…"
- "I'm doing it anyway"
- "It's good rice. I made it myself."
- The interlude's closing explanation

The best moments are the exceptions: the schedule exchange that ends on "Mm.", Akane's "…You always are.", the 水, the collar, and "Secret." Many of the cappers are required beats, so the fix is to strip the optional ones (c1_0125–0126, c1_0202, c1_0234) and let a few exchanges trail off or get interrupted. **[Problem 1]**

**Feelings stated, and narration vouching for dialogue.** It's contained, but it clusters in narration rather than speech: c1_0025, c1_0131, c1_0144, c1_0187, c1_0215, c1_0227 and c1_0234, all flagged above. The dialogue itself rarely states feelings. "She's nice, Kōhei. I like her." is the closest, and it works because the interlude showed her deciding it.

**Humor mechanisms (§3b).** These are mostly right. The leaks:
- Kōhei borrows a polished rival line (c1_0181) and, once, Nanoha's mock logic (c1_0238, acceptable).
- Ryōsuke and Rika both slip into dry irony in the rumor pair (c1_0125–0126).
- Public Nanoha gets two dry lines in a first meeting (c1_0193 and c1_0198).
- Natsuki's own mechanism, noticing physical details, is strong where it appears. But her exits are her weakest lines (c1_0199, c1_0224), and that is where the brief's request that she be as likable as Nanoha is lost.

**Narration joke density in 1.3–1.5.** Kōhei's narration is quippier than his speech. In a short span it has the cardigan, the uniform "ironed by someone who meant it," "elbow height," his hand that's "betrayed me," the weathervane, and the good rice. Each is fine alone, but together they give a punchline-every-paragraph rhythm that his short, plain spoken voice doesn't have. Trim two or three. **[Problem 2]**

**Tics:**
- **"Says it…" variants.** The dream's deliberate trio (the brief keeps it, so lint's "says it like ×2" flag is a known exception) has two cousins elsewhere: c1_0025 "She says it to the rice cooker" and c1_0215 "Nanoha said it as lightly as she knew how." With those, narration comments on line delivery five times in the chapter. (Counted with those two lines.)
- **Height gags.** Five: c1_0040, c1_0043, c1_0075, c1_0127 ("elbow height") and "Chibi" at c1_0256. "Short-person direction" and "Chibi" are required, so drop c1_0075 and the joke stops being milked. **[Problem 3]**
- **"Fine" appears six times:** c1_0017, c1_0090, c1_0200, c1_0227, c1_0228 and c1_0245. "It's fine" is Kōhei's signature dodge on the sheet, and it loses its edge when Nanoha's public line and her interlude narration use the same word. "She seems fine" and the interlude's "It was fine." may be meant as an echo. If so, keep those two and vary c1_0200. **[Problem 4]**
- **A repeated construction:** "Her hands stay a second longer than the collar strictly needs" (c1_0076) and "I stand there for a while longer than the garbage requires" (c1_0259). If the mirror is meant (she lingers, then he lingers), keep it. If not, change one. **[Problem 5]**
- **Face-reading.** Natsuki reads hands and posture, not his face, which is correct. Only c1_0224's "normal face" brushes against the tic.
- **"Very" and American-internet bits.** "Very" is fine (see the short version), and no American-internet bits remain; the taiyaki swap works.

**Note on the reader-knowledge target, not counted.** The dream's speaker ID is `past_natsuki`. If the build shows a name plate, or gives that line Natsuki's dialogue color (§3a) or her voice actor, the build points the dream at her no matter what the lines do. Check this in the build; I didn't read §3a.
