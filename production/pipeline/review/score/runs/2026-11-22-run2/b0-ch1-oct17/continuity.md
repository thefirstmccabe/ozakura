### 2. HIGH: Natsuki says *kimi* to Nanoha (pronoun, §3c)

### 3. MEDIUM: Natsuki says the past-life hint out loud on day one (reader-knowledge; touches an Open item)

Canon makes "how much she perceives, and when" Open (canon §6 Dating, line 349; §4 calendar, line 149), and the chapter map places her sensing at Ch27 (November).

- **Fix (preferred):** cut 321–330 and go straight from 320 to 331. **Or** swap in a probe with no past-life language that still shows her attention on him and lets Nanoha register it, e.g. Natsuki: "Is he always like that? He didn't turn a single page all first period." / Nanoha: "…He was listening." / Natsuki: "He wasn't." (This also uses Natsuki's §3b habit of noticing physical details.)

"Club doesn't start until Wednesday" fits Ch2's first practice, and the Saturday sweeping after practice fits Ch3 3.4–3.5. Akane's clinic day on the 18th is the existing [P].

- **Family facts:** Dad died "last spring," which matches his death around high-school entry. Food "for about a year" matches canon's FB6, where it starts after the funeral. Mom stays behind the fusuma (knock, no answer, creak). Akane's dizzy-spell tell appears and her diagnosis is never named. Nanoha has been next door "since we were born." Natsuki's father's job moves the family; the occupation is never given; "Sendai, most recently" matches the pilot list.
- **Open items kept open:** Kōhei's rank (he deflects the shodan question), Akane's diagnosis, both families' jobs, kanji (kana throughout apart from #17). POV follows the pilot's Working format (first-person present plus a third-person past-tense interlude).

- **Canon writing rules:** hair color is described plainly and nobody remarks on it. No manga self-reference. No name-tag intros (Rika arrives by tackle, Tokiwa mid-argument, both named by being addressed). Kōhei is warm and smiles easily.
- **Address:** apart from #1, #2 and #12, everything checks against §3c. Akane says "Onii-chan"/"Nanoha-chan." Nanoha says "Kōhei," with "Kō-chan" only in private (131, 365), "Akane-chan," "Rika-chan," and "Mizuno-san" → "Natsuki-san." Kōhei's narration uses "Tokiwa," "Mizuno" and "Nanoha," and the interlude follows Nanoha. Kōhei has a face window on every spoken line.
- **Staging:** entrances and exits are clean. Natsuki's seat behind Kōhei is consistent (237, 245, 255, 310). Nanoha is still in uniform with her bag at night, which fits her only now getting home. Music matches the outline.
- **Outline coverage:** all seven scenes are present and in order. Fixes 2, 4, 8–11, 13, 20, 25 and 26 are applied.

## Noted, not counted
- **First day of term:** in Japan it's usually the ceremony plus homeroom, ending around noon. Having first period, lunch and an after-school tour is manga license, and the approved outline has it, so it's fine if intended.
- **The year:** "Monday, April 7" plus canon's "smartphones everywhere, before generative AI" effectively pins it to 2014. Canon keeps the year Open, so keep dated tech and pop references vague.
- **Lunches:** Kōhei makes both lunches here. Canon 13c (Ch11) assumes "Nanoha has been making Kōhei's school lunch," so a later chapter needs to show that handover.
- **Line 319** (Natsuki: "Someone who knows which drawer sticks in your kitchen") foreshadows Ch4's scene-bank 3. It works as dramatic irony; just make sure Ch4 plays as her seeing it in person.
- **Natsuki's "second semesters" line** isn't in Ch1, and the outline doesn't require it. Line 331, "First days are usually…", stays compatible with "I'm amazing at first days" only if it's never finished as "lonely."
