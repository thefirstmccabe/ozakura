# Final answers

## When I became fairly sure (70%+)
- **That the dream is a past life tied to the present-day romance:** at image_07 together with "There's a dream I've had since I was small." (part 4). The kimono, real katana and cherry branch made it a period memory, and "She says it like she's been waiting a very long time" made it a love story.
- **That one of the two girls is the dream woman (rather than the tree spirit or someone new):** at "Her eyes are wide and confused, like she doesn't know where it came from either." (part 7).
- **Never reached 70% on *who* the dream woman is or *who* he ends up with.** The peaks were Natsuki 65% (dream) and Natsuki 60% (endgame), both at part 7. My endgame pick flipped three times (Nanoha → Natsuki → Nanoha → Natsuki), which I think is what the author wants after two chapters.

## Hints that felt heavy-handed, too obvious, or too coy
**Too obvious / heavy:**
1. **The "she reads him impossibly well" idea is stated three times in two chapters:** "I feel like I've met him somewhere before. I just can't place it." (part 3), "She was there before I moved." (part 6), and "i knew what he was going to do before he did it. every time" (part 8). Plus the tear. Each is fine alone, but together they're the same clue repeated rather than escalated. By the text to Chie I felt told.
2. **The tear on the catch (part 7)** is the textbook reincarnation beat. It's executed well, but it's the most recognizable move in the genre.
3. **Kōhei's "I've always been comfortable holding one. Since the first time I picked one up."** is a clue in a teenager's mouth. Nobody answers "what's the best part of kendo?" at lunch like that.
4. **The debana setup/payoff is too neat:** Natsuki names it as her favorite and says it's the hardest, Takatsuki confirms it's the hardest, and then she does it in the first exchange. I called it the moment she named it.

**Too coy:**
5. **The cold open (image_01–03).** It's wordless, uncaptioned, and never referred to again in two chapters: fog, a modern road with a traffic mirror, and a hand in a *modern zip jacket* holding a katana with the same hilt as the period dream. It doesn't read as a dream. Since the part-4 dream never mentions fog or a road, nothing bridges them. I genuinely don't know if it's the dream, a flash-forward, or something else, and the modern sleeve actively confused me about the dream's time period.
6. **Nanoha as dream-woman candidate goes stale.** Her case rests entirely on Chapter 1 temperament (caretaker, teasing, "waiting"). She gets no clue at all in Chapter 2. If she's meant to stay live, she needs one beat that rhymes with the dream.
7. **The garbage-day detail** ("Mon/Thu" in Dad's handwriting vs. "moved to Tuesdays and Fridays last fall… I went with the handwriting") is lovely but easy to miss. I had to flip back. This is the right side of coy, but some readers won't catch it.

**Right level (keep):** the wrist motif (水 on the inside of her wrist → dream cut on the inside of his wrist → kote), the hakama "off center for a year," and "Again."

## Other problems found
- **Pronoun bug, part 6:** "Two minutes later she's wiped the practice schedule off the whiteboard…" reads as Rika (the previous subject), but it's Natsuki. Use "Mizuno."
- **Translator's-note overload, part 6:** about 12 notes in one scene, six of them stacked between "After the break, bogu" and the match. That's the exact moment tension should build. The Rika lesson already teaches what a reader needs.
- **Taiyaki callback, part 5:** Natsuki asks "Head first or tail first?", but she wasn't present for Ryōsuke's taiyaki argument, and Kōhei answers "Don't start" as if it's their shared joke.
- **"水 (Wednesday)," part 3:** 水 means "water." The gloss should say it's short for Wednesday (水曜日), or many readers will be puzzled.
- **Cedar pollen "It's April" (parts 7–8):** I always understood Japanese hay fever to be a spring thing, so the rebuttal (and Chie repeating it) sounded wrong to me rather than funny. The joke needs either a different excuse or a line establishing the season is over.
- **Address wobble, part 4:** Nanoha says "Kō-chan" at the start and end of the scene but "She's nice, Kōhei. I like her." in the middle. That's fine if it's a deliberate serious-register shift, but nothing marks it.
- **Nanoha's absence from Chapter 2:** she's off screen except for the "Again." note. My attachment to her measurably dropped (6→5 wants, 50%→35% endgame). If the triangle is meant to stay balanced, she needs one live scene in Chapter 2.
