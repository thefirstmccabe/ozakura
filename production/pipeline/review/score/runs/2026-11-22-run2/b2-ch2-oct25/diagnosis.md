## 4. The five highest-value fixes (ranked)

1. **Stop explaining Natsuki on the bridge.** Answer "Why?" (0215) with something adjacent and concrete instead of the thesis (0216), cut the narration that vouches for and announces (0227–0228), and keep one walk-back (0226) instead of two.
2. **Let the author's kept exchange snap.** End her line on "Cedar pollen." so "It's April." answers it directly, and drop the "Late bloomers… Look it up" topper. The too-loud laugh (0202) is her reply.
3. **End c2_bridge on one or two beats.** Cut "It's only trash bags." and let Nanoha's lit window carry it. Have Story decide whether c2_tuesday also needs a Nanoha closer (0299), since two of them work against the chapter's evening-up job.
4. **Break the lesson's punchline-every-line rhythm.** Cut 0109 and 0128, and let at least one exchange hang or get ignored. Replace one of Rika's generic prompts with a topic jump or a specific obsession, so she isn't a question dispenser.
5. **Strip narration that pre-announces or captions:**
   - "half a second" (0141), so Kirishima reveals it.
   - The race clause (0179).
   - "lost-and-found" in 0090 (and fix its "she" pointing at Rika).
   - "Then they get careful with me." (0046).
   - "I understand it completely." (0163).
