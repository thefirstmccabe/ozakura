# Ōzakura — Chapter Map

**What this is:** the chapter-by-chapter plan for the whole story, built from canon (§4 calendar and pacing, §6 plot, §10 scene bank). `canon.md` governs; if they disagree, canon wins and this map gets fixed.

**Status:** first draft, Oct 12; **reworked Oct 18** for the author's Oct 18 decisions (rows, running threads, the reader windows, which became arcs on Nov 1, and the clue ledger below). Everything here is a **Proposal** (Claude) until the author approves it, except beats canon already decides. Chapter titles are working titles. **From Nov 25 the scene-level plan is `story/scene-list.md`** (every scene of the whole story, drafted for the author's approval before any production past Ch4). This map stays the chapter-level summary; where the two differ after he approves the scene list, the scene list is newer and this map gets fixed.

**Changes the author decided in his review of the scene list (Nov 25–26). The rows below are not rewritten yet:** they get rewritten Part by Part as he approves each Part of the scene list in full, so they aren't rewritten twice. Until then canon and `story/scene-list.md` (Pass 1, revised Nov 26) are current where a row differs.

**Shape:** a ~40-chapter spine (**42 since Oct 18; 44 since Oct 31**) on canon's working allocation (Oct 7; updated Oct 18 and Oct 31): 17 chapters April–August, 5 for the fight and September, 10 for October–December, 11 for January–March, 1 for the aftermath, plus the epilogue. Expandable daily-life chapters can be added later, first in the April–August stretch. **Decided Oct 12:** the 40-chapter spine; chapters run about 4,000–5,000 words where they cover a week or more (roughly 12–15 hours in all); slice-of-life and extra development added as needed.

**Reader-knowledge targets:** when the Story thread outlines a chapter for production, it adds what the reader should *suspect* and *know* by the chapter's end (style guide §9, step 1). **The three reader numbers** (who he ends up with, who readers want him to end up with, who the dream woman is) **are read at the end of each arc, as direction, not as limits** (author, Nov 1 and Nov 22; canon §6, "Reader targets"). Only the invariants are hard: nothing confirms the dream woman before the bloom (Ch32), and the ending isn't settled before he chooses. Balance is budgeted in each arc outline (whose chapters, how many uncanny beats each girl gets, Natsuki's present-day closeness), checked there against canon's three writing rules, and never handed to the scene writer as a percentage. Outlines don't add "balance beats." Fixes come only from canon's four tripwires, checked at arc ends. The Ch1–4 targets are at the end of `production/pilot-v2/scene-outline.md`.

**How Story applies plot decisions (plan approved by the author, Nov 22):**
- **Arc outline gate:** before an arc is built, the author approves one page: its turning points, what changes between the three leads, anything touching an Open item, and a short list of inventions he'd want to know about (a new character, a new recurring device, anything that shifts a relationship). Story fills in the scenes. Readers stress-test the arc page before anything is built.
- **Consequence trace:** before a plot decision is committed, a short list of every chapter-map row, clue-ledger entry, planned address shift and built line it touches, shown to the author when he decides.
- **Settling period** [P: a few days] before a plot decision is applied to approved chapters, and **one batch of backward changes per arc**, not one per decision.

**Running threads** (not repeated in every row):
- **Fairy (canon §8):** April–August, a drifting light about once a month that only Nanoha notices, plus small things moved in her room. Autumn, a half-form at the edge of her vision. New Year, full and speaking. January–March, visible to the whole group.
- **Shared-past flashbacks (canon §6):** FB1–FB6, one short scene each, triggered by something in the present.
- **Family:** Kōhei carries everything until September; Mom's partial recovery runs from September with setbacks.
- **Takatsuki (Kōhei's best friend and rival since Nov 21):** the cold open shows his January; in class from Ch1; a real rivalry between friends April–July (no chip on Kōhei's shoulder, author Nov 21), low point in September, learning from each other October–December, drawn in by the loop in January (Ch37). **No younger sibling (author, Nov 25):** Akane is the one with him in the cold open and in Ch37; the Nov 21 brother is removed. **Akane (author, Nov 25):** she's interested in him; he has some shared interest and isn't oblivious, but at the start she's completely off limits, his very protective best friend's sick little sister; that loosens a little, and there may be no confession. A small thread; placements are in the scene list. **He keeps the jobs he took from Ryōsuke on Nov 21** (the taiyaki entrance, the lunch scoring, the kaidan, game night at his place, the suspension; author, Nov 25).
- **Ryōsuke and Rika (Nov 25):** side schoolmates who are there when needed, not major characters. Ryōsuke is the class president, has no lines in Ch1, and is Kōhei and Takatsuki's third roommate in Kyoto. Rika is the gossipy, romance-minded friend who brings romance up with Nanoha (the confessions talk, Ch5; the Ch14 tease about "Mizuno"), gets kendo explained to her (Ch2), and is the girls' third roommate in Kyoto; she isn't one of the five or Nanoha's best friend.
- **Akane at their school (author, Nov 25):** a first-year, a year behind them; she can eat lunch with them; she's **Nanoha's romantic confidante**; "I want more Akane." **The core five (author, Nov 25):** Kōhei, Nanoha, Natsuki, Takatsuki and Akane; Natsuki doesn't start as one but becomes part of it. Placements come with the scene list and the arc outlines; rows written before Nov 25 still assume she's in middle school and Rika is one of the five (Ch5's outing "the five" with Rika's confessions talk, Ch11's lunches, Ch12's meet, Ch13's sleepover, Ch15), so each arc outline rechecks them.
- **Names (style guide §3c):** Kōhei's "Chibi" planted in Ch1–3; Nanoha → Natsuki "-chan" at the pact (Ch10); "It's Chibi. It's like eating at home" hurts (Ch11); Rika teases that Kōhei is still on "Mizuno" (Ch14); Akane → "Natsuki-chan" (Ch15); Takatsuki's one *omae* (Ch20); Natsuki's first "Kōhei" in her one voiced line (Ch19); Nanoha's cold "Fujisawa-kun" and "Natsuki-san" (Ch20); "Kōhei" back by Ch22; he calls her "Nanoha" as they start dating (Ch27); "Natsuki-chan" again (Ch28); she asks him to call her Natsuki at the farewell and he does (Ch33); Nanoha notices (Ch34), and later her plain "Natsuki" (Ch35).
- **Dread ramp (canon §2, Oct 18):** spring, local customs (Ch3, Ch4); summer, kaidan (Ch16); autumn, after the hiker: institutional dread and a deniable night walk (Ch28–30); Kōhei's recurring dreams at night (canon §6, Oct 24: her, it, the trap; placements below [P]) and, awake, past-life fight flashes only when his anger breaks loose (Ch12, Ch18). **Since Nov 25 (author) the legend stays a legend and this wolf doesn't follow it:** "just stopping for a rest" (Ch3) and the kaidan (Ch16) are kept, a fall brings no attack, and only Nanoha can get people out of its road (canon §6). Nothing new supernatural is added; the beast's own autumn beats stay canon's ordinary bad news. The trade is withheld until Ch42–43, and beast threads stay separate from past-life clues.
- **Present-day closeness between Natsuki and Kōhei (canon §6, Oct 21):** plenty of it, as the counterweight to Nanoha's flashback interiority. How much each arc gets is set in the arc outline (Nov 22); a chapter can belong to one girl (Nov 1), so not every chapter needs some. Its kind matters: with Natsuki he puts the load down (fun, rest, being sixteen; karaoke in Ch5, horror movies in Ch16), she's direct with him, and she isn't afraid of his edge. Nanoha's kind is sharing the load (the windows, the house, the flashbacks).
- **Kōhei's pull toward Natsuki (canon §6, Oct 18):** visible to the reader from the start (Ch2, Ch5, Ch9, Ch11, Ch14, Ch16, Ch26), avoided rather than missed; admitted to himself in Ch22; the choice in Ch27. His tie to Nanoha is carried by the windows, the shared-past flashbacks and the house.
- **Recurring dreams (canon §6, Oct 24) [P placements]:** her (the wrist): Ch1, Ch6 (fever), Ch20, and the wedding hood in Ch31; it (the eyes): Ch5, Ch16, Ch28, Ch30 (closest); the trap (the path): Ch11, Ch23, then real in Ch37. Roughly one every 3–4 chapters, never two in one chapter. **Nov 21:** the cold open shows the eyes in the mist, so the first eyes dream (Ch5) echoes it.
- **Supporting cast (canon §8, Oct 31):** each appears here and there with an interest, a running bit and a moment of depth; Kirishima stays just the captain. [P] placements so far: the guys' game night (Ch7), the girls' sleepover (Ch13), Chie's visit (Ch14), Takatsuki during the suspension he made him report (Ch20), Akane's plants and a scene where she takes care of Kōhei (to place), Rika's sketchbook (running).
- **Natsuki's silence (canon §6, Oct 18):** conviction after the wound care (Ch20); from then until Ch35 her scenes and interludes stay on what she does, never inside her thoughts (Ch20, Ch22, Ch26 as Nanoha's interlude, Ch28, Ch31, Ch32's face, Ch33 where Kōhei doesn't ask); the replay in Ch35 recasts them.
- **Past-life fragments:** small and physical all year; the substantial memories come at the New Year bloom. Reader-ahead past-life interludes are still Open (parked), so none are placed here.
