# Ōzakura — Chapter Map

**What this is:** the chapter-by-chapter plan for the whole story, built from canon (§4 calendar and pacing, §6 plot, §10 scene bank). `canon.md` governs; if they disagree, canon wins and this map gets fixed.

**Status:** first draft, Oct 12; **reworked Oct 18** for the author's Oct 18 decisions (rows, running threads, windows and clue ledger below). Everything here is a **Proposal** (Claude) until the author approves it, except beats canon already decides. Chapter titles are working titles.

**Shape:** a ~40-chapter spine (**42 since Oct 18**) on canon's working allocation (Oct 7; updated Oct 18): 17 chapters April–August, 5 for the fight and September, 8 for October–December, 11 for January–March, 1 for the aftermath, plus the epilogue. Expandable daily-life chapters can be added later, first in the April–August stretch. **Decided Oct 12:** the 40-chapter spine; chapters run about 4,000–5,000 words where they cover a week or more (roughly 12–15 hours in all); slice-of-life and extra development added as needed.

**Reader-knowledge targets (Oct 17):** when the Story thread outlines a chapter for production, it adds what the reader should *suspect* and *know* by the chapter's end (style guide §9, step 1). Cold readers measure each chapter against it. Ch1's cold reads (Oct 17) ended split on who the dream woman is (two readers leaned Nanoha, one Natsuki; none above 65%), with all three guessing wife or lover. **Decided Oct 18 (canon §5):** cold readers report three things every chapter: who he ends up with (about 50-50 until the Ch32 reaffirmation), who they want him to end up with (about 50-50), and who the dream woman is (Natsuki at most about 60% in the pilot, drifting up to about 65–70% before the bloom, never confirmed before Ch30; canon §5). The Story thread's Oct 17 Ch1–4 targets were superseded; the re-derived ones are at the end of `production/pilot-v2/scene-outline.md`.

**Running threads** (not repeated in every row):
- **Fairy (canon §8):** April–August, a drifting light about once a month that only Nanoha notices, plus small things moved in her room. Autumn, a half-form at the edge of her vision. New Year, full and speaking. January–March, visible to the whole group.
- **Shared-past flashbacks (canon §6):** FB1–FB6, one short scene each, triggered by something in the present.
- **Family:** Kōhei carries everything until September; Mom's partial recovery runs from September with setbacks.
- **Takatsuki:** friction April–July, low point in September, learning from each other October–December, drawn in by the loop in January.
- **Names (style guide §3c):** Kōhei's "Chibi" planted in Ch1–3; Nanoha → Natsuki "-chan" at the pact (Ch10); "It's Chibi. It's like eating at home" hurts (Ch11); Rika teases that Kōhei is still on "Mizuno" (Ch14); Akane → "Natsuki-chan" (Ch15); Takatsuki's one *omae* (Ch20); Natsuki's first "Kōhei" in her one voiced line (Ch19); Nanoha's cold "Fujisawa-kun" and "Natsuki-san" (Ch20); "Kōhei" back by Ch22; he calls her "Nanoha" as they start dating (Ch25); "Natsuki-chan" again (Ch26); she asks him to call her Natsuki at the farewell and he does (Ch31); Nanoha notices (Ch32), and later her plain "Natsuki" (Ch33).
- **Dread ramp (canon §2, Oct 18):** spring, local customs (Ch3, Ch4); summer, kaidan (Ch16); autumn, after the hiker: institutional dread and a deniable night walk (Ch26–28); Kōhei's fight fragments all year (Ch5, Ch12, Ch18, Ch28). Nothing new supernatural is added; the beast's own autumn beats stay canon's ordinary bad news. The trade is withheld until Ch40–41, and beast threads stay separate from past-life clues.
- **Kōhei's pull toward Natsuki (canon §6, Oct 18):** visible to the reader from the start (Ch2, Ch5, Ch9, Ch11, Ch14, Ch16, Ch24), avoided rather than missed; admitted to himself in Ch22; the choice in Ch25. His tie to Nanoha is carried by the windows, the shared-past flashbacks and the house.
- **Natsuki's silence (canon §6, Oct 18):** conviction after the wound care (Ch20); from then until Ch33 her scenes and interludes stay on what she does, never inside her thoughts (Ch20, Ch22, Ch24 as Nanoha's interlude, Ch26, Ch29, Ch30's face, Ch31 where Kōhei doesn't ask); the replay in Ch33 recasts them.
- **Past-life fragments:** small and physical all year; the substantial memories come at the New Year bloom. Reader-ahead past-life interludes are still Open (parked), so none are placed here.
