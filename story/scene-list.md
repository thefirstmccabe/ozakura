## Part 1 — Spring (April–May)
It opens in the winter to come (Chapter 0), then the opening credits, then April. Natsuki arrives, and from the first week both girls are plainly the love interests. Spring is the romcom at its lightest: Nanoha is already part of Kōhei's house, Natsuki gets him to put the load down, Akane starts at their school and wedges herself into the kendo club, the two boys are plainly best friends, and the five become a group. Kōhei says out loud the one thing he wants this year. Under it, the dream, a split second of snow at the tree and the light at Nanoha's window promise the other story.

## Part 2 — Rain and summer (June–August)
The rivalry comes into the open and stays friendly: an umbrella each girl cheats for, a pact made over a fever, two lunches on one desk. The first of the bad dreams comes with the first storm. Summer is the cast at its happiest and the triangle at full tilt, with Kōhei feeling the pull toward Natsuki and, at the fireworks, looking. The one thing he wants for himself, the late-summer qualifier, is being eaten by his shifts. Akane's place in the club costs her a bad day at the summer meet, where his anger first shows. The season ends quietly, at Obon, before the night that breaks it.
