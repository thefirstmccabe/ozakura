# Ōzakura scene list, Pass 1

**Lede:** The whole story at one line per scene, for you to react to the shape. Nothing here is approved yet.

**Status (Nov 25):** a draft by the scene-list thread (Story role), from canon, the chapter map and the author's Nov 25 decisions. **No production past Ch4 starts until the author approves this.** Pass 2 will write each Part in full (when, where, who, what happens, what changes, threads, on screen as) and run it past outline readers before it goes to him.

## How to read this
- **IDs never renumber.** A scene added later gets a letter (12.3a); a cut scene leaves a gap. `P.0` is the cold open, `V1`–`V4` are past-life vignettes, `E.1` is the epilogue.
- **The italic line under each chapter:** when it happens; whose chapter it is (Nanoha's, Natsuki's, shared or neither); how much present-day closeness Natsuki and Kōhei get (none, light, strong); who is in it (K Kōhei, Na Nanoha, Nt Natsuki, T Takatsuki, A Akane, then side cast); and any uncanny or dread beat with its owner.
- **Tags at the end of a scene,** in this order: whose scene (Nanoha or Natsuki; none means neither); how it's shown if not plain sprites (CG, CG×2, insert = manga insert, gag = gag insert in a different art style, still = living still, anim = a short animation attempt, imagined = an imagined scene); threads (comic, dread, A+T = Akane and Takatsuki, family, kendo, uncanny); and the source.
- **Sources:** Built (in the built Ch1–3), D (decided by the author or in canon as Decided), SB and a number (canon's scene bank), Map (the chapter map's proposal, which he has seen but not approved), **P (invented in this pass)**. A scene tagged both Built and P is built and has a proposed change.
- **"Your call"** under a scene is a question only the author can answer. Open items in canon are proposed here, never settled.
- The review page and the tables at the end are generated from this file by `production/scene-list/build_page.py`. Keep the line shapes.

## Part 1 — Spring (April–May)
Natsuki arrives, and from the first week both girls are plainly the love interests. Spring is the romcom at its lightest: Nanoha is already part of Kōhei's house, Natsuki gets him to put the load down, Akane starts at their school and wedges herself into the kendo club, and the five become a group. Under it, the dream, the light at the tree and the first bad dream promise the other story.

## Part 2 — Rain and summer (June–August)
The rivalry comes into the open and stays friendly: an umbrella each girl cheats for, a pact made over a fever, two lunches on one desk. Summer is the cast at its happiest and the triangle at full tilt, with Kōhei feeling the pull toward Natsuki and looking away from it. Akane's place in the club costs her a bad day at the summer meet, where his anger first shows. The season ends quietly, at Obon, before the night that breaks it.

## Part 3 — The fight and September (late August–September)
One night undoes the summer. After a tournament he knows he should have done better in, Kōhei beats a man who harassed Nanoha, knocks her down by accident and frightens her; he ends up at Natsuki's door, and the kiss breaks the girls' pact.
